# Ignatius to the Smyrnaeans

Chapters: [1](#chapter-1) · [2](#chapter-2) · [3](#chapter-3) · [4](#chapter-4) · [5](#chapter-5) · [6](#chapter-6) · [7](#chapter-7) · [8](#chapter-8) · [9](#chapter-9) · [10](#chapter-10) · [11](#chapter-11) · [12](#chapter-12) · [13](#chapter-13)

<sup>1</sup> From Ignatius, also called Theophorus[^0-1-1], to the church of God the Father and of his beloved Jesus Christ in Smyrna of Asia: a church that has received mercy in every gift, been filled with faith and love, and lacks no gift; most worthy of God, and bearing what is holy. Abundant greetings in a blameless spirit and in the word of God.

## Chapter 1

<sup>1</sup> I give glory to Jesus Christ, God, who has made you so wise. I perceive that you are fitted out with unmoving faith, as if nailed to the cross of the Lord Jesus Christ in flesh and spirit, and firmly established in love through the blood of Christ. You are wholly sure about our Lord: he is truly from David’s line in the flesh, God’s Son by God’s will and power, truly born from a virgin, baptized by John so that all righteousness would be fulfilled[^1-1-1] by him,

<sup>2</sup> he was truly nailed to a cross in the flesh on our behalf, in the time of Pontius Pilate and Herod the tetrarch. We are fruit of that cross[^1-2-1] and of his divinely blessed suffering, so that through the resurrection he might lift up a standard[^1-2-2] for all time for his holy and faithful ones, Jews and gentiles alike, in the single body of his church.

## Chapter 2

<sup>1</sup> He suffered all of this for our sake, so that we might be saved; and he truly suffered, just as he truly raised himself. It is not as some unbelievers say, that he only seemed to suffer (they themselves only seem to exist). And as they think, so it will turn out for them: they will be without bodies, like demons.

## Chapter 3

<sup>1</sup> I know and believe that he was in the flesh even after the resurrection.

<sup>2</sup> When he came to those around Peter, he said to them: “Take hold of me[^3-2-1], touch me, and see that I am not a bodiless demon[^3-2-2].” They touched him at once and believed, joined to his flesh and spirit. This is why they despised death and were found to be above death.

<sup>3</sup> After the resurrection he ate and drank with them as a being of flesh, although in spirit he was united with the Father.

## Chapter 4

<sup>1</sup> I give you this counsel, beloved, knowing you are of the same mind. I am putting you on guard against the beasts that wear human shape. Not only should you refuse to take them in; if possible you should not even run into them. Simply pray for them, in the hope that they will somehow repent, though that is difficult. Jesus Christ, our true life, has power over it. If the Lord did these things in appearance only, I too am in chains in appearance only. And why have I surrendered myself to death, to fire, to the sword, to beasts? Because near the sword is near God, and among the beasts is among God. In the name of Jesus Christ alone I endure every hardship in order to suffer with him, for he, the perfect human being[^4-1-1], empowers me.

## Chapter 5

<sup>1</sup> Some deny him out of ignorance, or rather were denied by him, advocates of death rather than of truth. Neither the prophecies nor the law of Moses has persuaded them, nor, up to now, the gospel, nor our own individual sufferings.

<sup>2</sup> They think the same about us. What good does it do me if someone praises me but slanders my Lord by not confessing that he bore flesh? Whoever says this has completely denied him, and is a carrier of a corpse.

<sup>3</sup> I did not think it right to write down their names, since they are unbelievers. May I not even remember them until they repent and turn to the passion, which is our resurrection.

## Chapter 6

<sup>1</sup> No one should be deceived. Heavenly beings, the glory of the angels, the rulers seen and unseen: they too face judgment unless they believe in the blood of Christ. Let the one who can take this in take it in[^6-1-1]. Let rank puff up no one, because faith and love are the whole thing, and nothing is ranked above them.

<sup>2</sup> Observe those with different beliefs about the grace of Jesus Christ that has come to us, and notice how contrary to God’s mind they are. They are not concerned about love, or the widow, the orphan, the person in distress, the prisoner or the one set free, the hungry or the thirsty.

## Chapter 7

<sup>1</sup> They abstain from eucharist and prayer, because they do not acknowledge that the eucharist is the flesh of our Savior Jesus Christ, flesh that suffered for our sins and that the Father, in his goodness, raised up. So those who dispute God’s gift die while arguing. It would be better for them to have love, so that they too could rise.

<sup>2</sup> It is right to keep far from such people, and neither privately nor publicly to talk about them, but to heed the prophets, and especially the gospel, where the passion is made known to us and the resurrection is fulfilled. Run from divisions, for they are the source of evils.

## Chapter 8

<sup>1</sup> Follow the bishop, all of you, as Jesus Christ followed the Father, and the presbytery as the apostles. Respect the deacons as God’s commandment. Let no one do anything that concerns the church apart from the bishop. Let that be considered a valid Eucharist which is held under the bishop or by someone he authorizes.

<sup>2</sup> Where the bishop appears, there let the congregation be, just as where Jesus Christ is, there is the whole church[^8-2-1]. Without the bishop it is not allowed either to baptize or to hold a love feast[^8-2-2]. Whatever he approves is also pleasing to God, so that whatever you do may be reliable and firm.

## Chapter 9

<sup>1</sup> It is reasonable, finally, for us to come to our senses while we still have time to repent toward God. It is good to know God and the bishop. Whoever honors the bishop has been honored by God; whoever does anything behind the bishop’s back serves the devil.

<sup>2</sup> May everything abound for you in grace, for you deserve it. You refreshed me in every way, and may Jesus Christ refresh you. You loved me when I was absent and when I was present. God is your reward; by enduring everything for his sake you will reach him.

## Chapter 10

<sup>1</sup> You did well to welcome Philo and Rheus Agathopus, who followed me for the word of God, as servants of God. They too thank the Lord for you, because you refreshed them in every way. Nothing will be lost for you.

<sup>2</sup> My spirit and my chains are given for you[^10-2-1], chains you did not look down on or feel ashamed of. The perfect hope, Jesus Christ, will not be ashamed of you either.

## Chapter 11

<sup>1</sup> Your prayer has reached the church in Antioch in Syria. From there, in chains most fitting to God, I greet everyone, though I am not worthy to be from there, being the least of them. By God’s will I was counted worthy, not from my own awareness of worth but by God’s grace, which I pray will be given to me fully, so that through your prayer I may reach God.

<sup>2</sup> To bring your work to completion on earth and in heaven, you should, to honor God, elect from your church a delegate for God, who will travel to Syria and rejoice with them that they are at peace, that they have regained their own greatness, and that their own community has been restored to them.

<sup>3</sup> It struck me as a thing worthy of God to send one of your members with a letter, to join in glorifying the fair weather God has granted them, because they have already reached the harbor, thanks to your prayer. You are perfect, so think perfect thoughts. When you are willing to act well, God stands ready to supply what you need.

## Chapter 12

<sup>1</sup> Greetings come from the love of the brothers and sisters[^12-1-1] at Troas. I am writing to you from there by the hand of Burrhus, whom you and the Ephesians, your own brothers and sisters, sent with me and who has given me comfort at every turn. I wish that everyone imitated him, since he is a pattern of what service to God is. May grace repay him in everything.

<sup>2</sup> My greetings to your bishop, so worthy of God, to your presbytery, suited to God, to my fellow slaves the deacons, and to all of you, singly and together, in the name of Jesus Christ, in his flesh and blood, his passion and resurrection, which are both physical and spiritual, in the unity of God and of you. Grace be yours, mercy, peace, and perseverance at all times.

## Chapter 13

<sup>1</sup> Greetings to the households of my brothers and sisters, wives and children included, and to the virgins who are called widows[^13-1-1]. Be strong in the Father’s power. Philo, who is with me, sends you his greeting.

<sup>2</sup> Greetings to Tavia’s household; my prayer is that she will be firmly grounded in faith and love, both of the flesh and of the spirit. Greetings to Alke, a name I long to hear, to Daphnus, who has no equal, to Eutecnus, and to every person by name. Be strong in God’s grace.

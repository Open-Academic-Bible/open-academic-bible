# Didache

*The Teaching of the Twelve Apostles* is a short church manual, probably from Syria or Palestine in the late first or early second century. It sets out the "two ways" of life and death and gives instructions on baptism, fasting, prayer, the thanksgiving meal (the Eucharist), travelling apostles and prophets, and the choice of leaders. Eusebius (early fourth century) lists it among the disputed books. Athanasius, in his Easter letter of 367, places it outside the canon but among the books to be read by new converts. The full Greek text was lost until 1873, when it was found in a manuscript dated 1056. That manuscript breaks off at 16:8.

## Chapter 1

<sup>1</sup> There are two ways, one of life and one of death, and the two ways are very different.

<sup>2</sup> The way of life, then, is this: First, you shall love the God who made you; second, your neighbor as yourself.[^1-2-1] And whatever you would not wish done to you, you too must not do to another.[^1-2-2]

<sup>3</sup> Now this is what these words teach: Bless those who curse you and pray for your enemies; fast on behalf of your persecutors. For what merit is there in loving those who love you? Do not even the gentiles do the same? But as for you, love the ones who hate you, and you will have no enemy.[^1-3-1]

<sup>4</sup> Keep away from the cravings of the flesh and the body. If someone strikes you on the right cheek, turn to him the other one too, and you will be perfect.[^1-4-1] If someone presses you into service for one mile, go two with him. If someone takes your cloak, give him your tunic too. If someone takes what is yours, do not demand its return, for you cannot.[^1-4-2]

<sup>5</sup> Give to all who ask of you, and do not ask for its return, for the Father wants everyone to receive from the gifts that are his own. Blessed is the giver who follows the commandment, for that one is guiltless. Woe to the receiver! If someone takes because of need, they are guiltless; but whoever takes without need will answer for why they took and for what. Placed under arrest, they will be questioned about their deeds and will not be released until they repay the final penny.[^1-5-1]

<sup>6</sup> But about this it has also been said: “Let your alms sweat in your hands until you know who should receive it.”[^1-6-1]

## Chapter 2

<sup>1</sup> Now the teaching’s second commandment:

<sup>2</sup> You shall not murder; you shall not commit adultery; you shall not corrupt boys;[^2-2-1] you shall not commit sexual immorality; you shall not steal; you shall not use magic; you shall not mix potions;[^2-2-2] you shall not kill a child by abortion, nor kill one that has been born; you shall not covet what belongs to your neighbor.

<sup>3</sup> You shall not swear falsely; you shall not bear false witness; you shall not slander; you shall not bear malice.

<sup>4</sup> You shall not be of two minds or of two tongues, for being double-tongued is a snare of death.

<sup>5</sup> Your speech shall not be false or empty, but backed by what you do.[^2-5-1]

<sup>6</sup> You shall not be greedy, or grasping, or a hypocrite, or spiteful, or proud. You shall not form an evil plan against your neighbor.

<sup>7</sup> You shall not hate anyone; but some you shall reprove, for others you shall pray, and others you shall love more than your own soul.[^2-7-1]

## Chapter 3

<sup>1</sup> My child, flee from every evil thing and from whatever is like it.

<sup>2</sup> Do not be quick to anger, for anger leads the way to murder; nor jealous,[^3-2-1] nor quarrelsome, nor hot-tempered, for murders are born of all these.

<sup>3</sup> My child, do not be full of desire, for desire leads the way to sexual immorality; nor foul-mouthed, nor one whose eyes wander,[^3-3-1] for adulteries are born of all these.

<sup>4</sup> My child, do not read omens,[^3-4-1] for it leads the way to idolatry; nor be an enchanter, nor an astrologer, nor one who performs purification rites;[^3-4-2] do not even wish to look at these things,[^3-4-3] for idolatry is born of all these.

<sup>5</sup> My child, do not be a liar, for lying leads the way to theft; nor a lover of money, nor vain, for thefts are born of all these.

<sup>6</sup> My child, do not be a grumbler, for it leads the way to blasphemy; nor self-willed, nor evil-minded, for blasphemies are born of all these.

<sup>7</sup> Be meek, rather, for the meek will inherit the earth.[^3-7-1]

<sup>8</sup> Be patient and merciful, without malice, calm and good, and always in awe of[^3-8-1] the words you have heard.

<sup>9</sup> You shall not exalt yourself or let your soul grow insolent. Your soul shall not join itself to the proud, but you shall live among the righteous and the lowly.

<sup>10</sup> Receive the things that befall you as good, knowing that without God nothing happens.

## Chapter 4

<sup>1</sup> My child, call to mind night and day the one who speaks God’s word to you, and honor him as the Lord. For where the Lord’s authority[^4-1-1] is spoken of, there the Lord is.

<sup>2</sup> Seek the faces of the saints[^4-2-1] every day, so that their words may give you rest.

<sup>3</sup> You shall not desire division but shall reconcile those who quarrel. You shall judge justly; you shall not favor anyone when you correct faults.

<sup>4</sup> You shall not waver over whether it will happen or not.[^4-4-1]

<sup>5</sup> Do not be someone who stretches out the hands to receive and draws them in to give.

<sup>6</sup> If you have gained anything by your hands, you shall give it as redemption[^4-6-1] for your sins.

<sup>7</sup> You shall not hesitate to give, nor grumble while giving, for you will come to know who is the good paymaster of the reward.

<sup>8</sup> You shall not turn your back on the needy, but shall share all things with your brother[^4-8-1] and not call anything your own.[^4-8-2] For if you are sharers in what is undying, how much more in things that die!

<sup>9</sup> You shall not keep your hand from[^4-9-1] your son or your daughter, but shall teach them the fear of God from their youth.

<sup>10</sup> You shall not give orders in bitterness to your slave or servant girl, who hope in the same God, lest they stop fearing the God who is over you both; for he does not come to call people according to their standing, but those whom the Spirit has prepared.

<sup>11</sup> And you slaves, be subject to your masters as to an image of God,[^4-11-1] with respect and fear.

<sup>12</sup> You shall hate all hypocrisy and everything that does not please the Lord.

<sup>13</sup> You shall never abandon the commandments of the Lord, but shall keep what you received, neither adding nor taking away.[^4-13-1]

<sup>14</sup> In the assembly[^4-14-1] you shall confess your wrongdoings, and you shall not go to your prayer with a bad conscience. This is the way of life.

## Chapter 5

<sup>1</sup> And this is the way of death. Before all else it is evil and full of cursing: murders, adulteries, lusts, acts of sexual immorality, thefts, idolatries, magic arts, sorceries, robberies, false testimonies, hypocrisies, double-heartedness, deceit, pride, malice, self-will, greed, foul talk, jealousy, insolence, haughtiness, boastfulness.

<sup>2</sup> Persecutors of the good, haters of truth, lovers of falsehood, not knowing the reward of righteousness, not holding fast to the good or to just judgment, awake not for good but for evil; far from them are gentleness and endurance; they love empty things, chase after payment, show no mercy to the poor, do not labor for the oppressed, do not know the one who made them; murderers of children, destroyers of what God has formed, they turn away the needy, oppress the afflicted, champion the rich, judge the poor lawlessly, and are sinful through and through. May you be rescued, children, from all these!

## Chapter 6

<sup>1</sup> Watch that no one turns you aside from this way of the teaching, for that person’s teaching is apart from God.

<sup>2</sup> For if you are able to carry the Lord’s yoke in full, you will be perfect;[^6-2-1] if you are not able, do what you are able to do.

<sup>3</sup> And about food, bear what you are able; but be very careful about what has been sacrificed to idols, for it is the worship of dead gods.

## Chapter 7

<sup>1</sup> Concerning baptism, baptize this way: having first said all these things,[^7-1-1] baptize into the name of the Father and of the Son and of the Holy Spirit, in living water.[^7-1-2]

<sup>2</sup> If you do not have living water, baptize in other water; and if you cannot in cold, then in warm.

<sup>3</sup> If you have neither, pour water three times on the head into the name of Father and Son and Holy Spirit.

<sup>4</sup> Before the baptism, let the one who baptizes and the one being baptized fast, and any others who can; and you shall tell the one being baptized to fast one or two days before.

## Chapter 8

<sup>1</sup> Do not let your fasts be with the hypocrites, for they fast on the second and the fifth day of the week;[^8-1-1] but you are to fast on the fourth day and on the day of preparation.[^8-1-2]

<sup>2</sup> And do not pray as the hypocrites do, but pray this way, as the Lord commanded in his gospel: “Our Father in heaven, hallowed be your name. Your kingdom come, your will be done, on earth as it is in heaven. Give us this day our daily bread.[^8-2-1] And forgive us our debt, as we also forgive our debtors. And do not bring us to the time of testing,[^8-2-2] but deliver us from the evil one.[^8-2-3] For yours is the power and the glory forever.”[^8-2-4]

<sup>3</sup> Pray this way three times a day.

## Chapter 9

<sup>1</sup> Concerning the thanksgiving,[^9-1-1] give thanks this way:

<sup>2</sup> First, concerning the cup: “We thank you, our Father, for the holy vine of your servant David,[^9-2-1] which you revealed to us through your servant Jesus. Glory be to you forever.”

<sup>3</sup> And concerning the broken bread: “We thank you, our Father, for the life and the knowledge you revealed to us through your servant Jesus. Glory be to you forever.

<sup>4</sup> As this broken bread was scattered over the mountains and, when gathered, became one, so let your church be gathered from the ends of the earth into your kingdom. For glory and power are yours through Jesus Christ forever.”

<sup>5</sup> No one is to eat or drink of your thanksgiving except those baptized into the Lord’s name; for about this too the Lord has said, “Do not give what is holy to the dogs.”[^9-5-1]

## Chapter 10

<sup>1</sup> After you have eaten your fill, give thanks this way:

<sup>2</sup> “We thank you, holy Father, for your holy name, which you made to dwell[^10-2-1] in our hearts, and for the knowledge, the faith, and the immortality you revealed to us through your servant Jesus. Glory be to you forever.

<sup>3</sup> You, Master Almighty, made everything because of your name, and you gave people food and drink for their enjoyment, that they might thank you; but on us you have bestowed spiritual food and drink and life eternal through your servant.[^10-3-1]

<sup>4</sup> Above all we thank you that you are mighty. Glory be to you forever.

<sup>5</sup> Remember, Lord, your church: deliver it from every evil and make it perfect in your love; and gather it, sanctified, from the four winds into the kingdom you have made ready for it. For yours is the power and yours the glory forever.

<sup>6</sup> Let grace come, and let this world pass away. Hosanna to the God of David![^10-6-1] If anyone is holy, let them come; if anyone is not, let them repent. Maranatha![^10-6-2] Amen.”

<sup>7</sup> But allow the prophets to give thanks as much as they wish.[^10-7-1]

## Chapter 11

<sup>1</sup> So whoever comes and teaches you all these things said above, receive him.

<sup>2</sup> But if the teacher himself turns aside and teaches another teaching that tears this down, do not listen to him; but if he teaches so as to add righteousness and knowledge of the Lord, receive him as the Lord.

<sup>3</sup> Concerning apostles and prophets, act this way, as the gospel directs.

<sup>4</sup> Every apostle who comes to you should be received as the Lord.

<sup>5</sup> He shall stay only one day, or a second if there is need; if he stays three, he is a false prophet.

<sup>6</sup> When the apostle goes on his way, let him take nothing but bread to last until he finds lodging for the night. If he asks for money, he is a false prophet.

<sup>7</sup> Do not test or judge any prophet who is speaking in the Spirit, for every sin will be forgiven, but this sin will not be forgiven.[^11-7-1]

<sup>8</sup> Yet not everyone who speaks in the Spirit is a prophet, only one who has the ways of the Lord. So by their ways the false prophet and the prophet will be known.

<sup>9</sup> No prophet who orders a meal in the Spirit eats from it; if he does, he is a false prophet.

<sup>10</sup> Every prophet who teaches the truth but does not do what he teaches is a false prophet.

<sup>11</sup> But any prophet who has been tested and is true, who acts for the worldly mystery of the church[^11-11-1] but does not teach others to do all that he does, shall not be judged by you, for his judgment rests with God; the prophets of old also acted in the same way.

<sup>12</sup> But whoever says in the Spirit, “Give me silver,” or something else, you shall not listen to him; but if he tells you to give for others who are in want, no one should judge him.

## Chapter 12

<sup>1</sup> Let everyone who comes in the name of the Lord be received; but afterward test him and you will know him, for you will have understanding of right and left.[^12-1-1]

<sup>2</sup> If the one who comes is a traveler passing through, help him all you can; but he shall stay with you no more than two or three days, if there is need.

<sup>3</sup> If he wants to settle among you and has a trade, let him work and eat.

<sup>4</sup> If he has no trade, use your understanding to see that he does not live among you as an idle Christian.

<sup>5</sup> If he will not do this, he is making a trade of Christ.[^12-5-1] Keep away from such people.

## Chapter 13

<sup>1</sup> But every true prophet who wants to settle among you deserves his food.

<sup>2</sup> So too a true teacher, like the laborer, himself deserves his food.[^13-2-1]

<sup>3</sup> So you shall take every firstfruit of the produce of winepress and threshing floor, of cattle and of sheep, and give the firstfruit to the prophets, for they are your high priests.

<sup>4</sup> But if you have no prophet, give to the poor.

<sup>5</sup> If you bake bread, take the firstfruit and give as the commandment says.

<sup>6</sup> In the same way, when you open a jar of wine or of oil, take the firstfruit and give it to the prophets.

<sup>7</sup> Of money, of clothing, and of every possession, take the firstfruit as seems right to you, and give as the commandment says.

## Chapter 14

<sup>1</sup> On the Lord’s day of the Lord,[^14-1-1] come together, break the bread, and give thanks,[^14-1-2] after first confessing your wrongdoings, so that your sacrifice is pure.

<sup>2</sup> Let no one who has a dispute with a companion join you until they are reconciled, lest your sacrifice be made unclean.

<sup>3</sup> For this is the sacrifice the Lord spoke of: “In every place and time offer me a pure sacrifice, for I am a great king, says the Lord, and my name is wonderful among the nations.”[^14-3-1]

## Chapter 15

<sup>1</sup> So choose[^15-1-1] for yourselves overseers[^15-1-2] and deacons worthy of the Lord, men who are gentle, not lovers of money, truthful, and tested; for they too serve you in the ministry of the prophets and teachers.

<sup>2</sup> So do not look down on them, for they are the ones among you who are honored, together with the prophets and teachers.

<sup>3</sup> Reprove one another, not in anger but peaceably, as the gospel has it. And if anyone wrongs another, let no one talk with them, nor let them hear a word from you, until they repent.

<sup>4</sup> Your prayers and your almsgiving and everything you do, do them all as you have it in the gospel of our Lord.

## Chapter 16

<sup>1</sup> Keep watch over your life. Let your lamps not be quenched nor your belts come loose,[^16-1-1] but be ready, since you do not know at what hour our Lord comes.[^16-1-2]

<sup>2</sup> Come together often, seeking what is good for your souls, for the whole time of your faith will be no help to you unless you are made perfect at the last hour.

<sup>3</sup> For in the last days false prophets and corrupters will multiply, and the sheep will turn into wolves, and love will turn into hate.

<sup>4</sup> As lawlessness grows, people will hate one another, persecute, and hand one another over.[^16-4-1] Then the deceiver of the world will appear as a son of God; he will do signs and wonders,[^16-4-2] the earth will be handed over into his power, and he will do unspeakable things such as have never been done since the beginning of time.

<sup>5</sup> Then human creation will come into the fire of testing, and many will stumble and be lost; but those who endure in their faith will be saved by the accursed one himself.[^16-5-1]

<sup>6</sup> And then the signs of the truth will appear: first, the sign of an opening in heaven;[^16-6-1] next, the sign of a trumpet’s sound;[^16-6-2] and third, the resurrection of the dead—

<sup>7</sup> yet not of all, but as it was said, “The Lord will come, and all the holy ones with him.”[^16-7-1]

<sup>8</sup> Then the world will see the Lord coming on the clouds of heaven.[^16-8-1]

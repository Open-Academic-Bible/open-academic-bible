# Letter to the Laodiceans

A short letter in Paul's name, made up largely of phrases from Philippians and Galatians, apparently written to supply "the letter from Laodicea" mentioned in Col 4:16. Its date is uncertain (second to fourth century). It is included in the Latin Bible of Codex Fuldensis (546) and in more than a hundred later Latin Bible manuscripts, and it was printed in some vernacular Bibles before the Reformation. It survives in Latin.

## Chapter 1

<sup>1</sup> Paul, an apostle sent neither by human authority nor through any human being[^1-1-1], but through Jesus Christ, to the brothers and sisters who live in Laodicea.

<sup>2</sup> Grace and peace to you from God the Father and the Lord Jesus Christ.

<sup>3</sup> I give thanks to Christ in every prayer I offer, because you remain in him, keep steadily at his works, and wait for the promise on the day of judgment.

<sup>4</sup> Do not let the empty talk of certain people who worm their way in leave you without support[^1-4-1], as they try to turn you away from the truth of the gospel that I proclaim.

<sup>5</sup> And now God will bring it about that those who come from me[^1-5-1] serve for the advance of the truth of the gospel and carry out kind deeds that lead to the salvation of eternal life.

<sup>6</sup> And now my chains are in plain view, which I bear in Christ; in them I am glad and rejoice.

<sup>7</sup> This is a source of lasting salvation for me, and it comes about through your prayers and the help of the Holy Spirit[^1-7-1], whether by life or by death.

<sup>8</sup> For to me, living is in Christ[^1-8-1], and dying is joy.

<sup>9</sup> By his mercy he will do the same in you[^1-9-1], so that you have the same love and are of one mind[^1-9-2].

<sup>10</sup> So then, dear friends, as you heard when I was present with you[^1-10-1], hold to it and act in the fear of God[^1-10-2], and you will have life for ever.

<sup>11</sup> For it is God who is at work in you[^1-11-1].

<sup>12</sup> And do whatever you do without holding back[^1-12-1].

<sup>13</sup> For the rest, dear friends, rejoice in Christ[^1-13-1], and be on guard against people who are base in their pursuit of profit[^1-13-2].

<sup>14</sup> Let all your requests be open before God[^1-14-1], and stand firm in the mind of Christ.

<sup>15</sup> And whatever is upright[^1-15-1], true, modest, just, and lovable[^1-15-2], do it.

<sup>16</sup> And what you have heard and received, keep in your heart, and you will have peace[^1-16-1].

<sup>17</sup> Greet all the brothers and sisters with a holy kiss[^1-17-1].

<sup>18</sup> The saints greet you.

<sup>19</sup> The grace of the Lord Jesus[^1-19-1] be with your spirit.

<sup>20</sup> And see that it is read to the Colossians[^1-20-1], and that the Colossians’ letter[^1-20-2] is read to you.

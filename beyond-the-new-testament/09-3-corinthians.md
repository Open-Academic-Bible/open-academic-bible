# 3 Corinthians

Chapters: [1](#chapter-1) · [2](#chapter-2) · [3](#chapter-3)

## Chapter 1

… prayed[^1-0-1] that a messenger be sent to Philippi. The Corinthians were deeply troubled about Paul, because he was about to leave this world before his time. Two men, Simon and Cleobius, had come to Corinth and were saying, “There is no resurrection of the flesh, only of the spirit. The human body is not God’s creation. As for the world, God did not make it, and God does not know it. Jesus Christ was not crucified, but only seemed to be; and he was not born of Mary or of the seed of David.” In short, they taught many things in Corinth, misleading many others, and themselves as well. So when the Corinthians heard that Paul was in Philippi, they sent a letter to him in Macedonia with the deacons Threptus and Eutychus. The letter ran as follows:

<sup>1</sup> Stephanus[^1-1-2] and the elders with him, Daphnus, Eubulus, Theophilus, and Zeno, to Paul their brother[^1-1-1]: eternal greetings in the Lord.

<sup>2</sup> Two men, Simon and Cleobius, have come to Corinth and are shaking the faith of many with their corrupt words.

<sup>3</sup> Please test and examine what they say.

<sup>4</sup> We have never heard words like these, either from you or from the rest of the apostles.

<sup>5</sup> Everything we received from you, or from them, we hold on to.

<sup>6</sup> The Lord has had mercy on us, so that while you are still in the flesh we may hear these things from you once more;

<sup>7</sup> so we ask that you come to us, if you can, or else write to us.

<sup>8</sup> For we believe, as it was disclosed to Theonoe, that the Lord has delivered you from the hand of the lawless one[^1-8-1].

<sup>9</sup> These are the things that those men say and teach.

<sup>10</sup> They say that we should not make use of the prophets,

<sup>11</sup> that God is not almighty,

<sup>12</sup> that there will be no resurrection of the flesh,

<sup>13</sup> that humankind was not made by God,

<sup>14</sup> that Christ did not come in the flesh and was not born of Mary,

<sup>15</sup> and that the world belongs not to God but to angels.

<sup>16</sup> So, brother[^1-16-1], we beg you to make every effort to come to us, so that the church of the Corinthians may stay free from offense and the folly of these men be exposed. Farewell always in the Lord.

## Chapter 2

<sup>1</sup> Threptus and Eutychus, the deacons, took the letter to Philippi,

<sup>2</sup> and Paul received it while he was in chains because of Stratonice[^2-2-1], the wife of Apollophanes. He forgot his chains and was deeply distressed,

<sup>3</sup> and he cried out, “It would be better for me to die and be with the Lord than to remain in the flesh and hear such things, the disasters of false teaching, so that sorrow is heaped on sorrow.

<sup>4</sup> On top of all this great affliction, I am in chains and I see these evils[^2-4-1], through which Satan’s schemes are carried out.”

<sup>5</sup> So Paul, in great distress, wrote a letter in reply, as follows:

## Chapter 3

<sup>1</sup> From Paul, who is held prisoner for Jesus Christ, to the brothers and sisters at Corinth: greetings.

<sup>2</sup> Surrounded as I am by many trials, I am not surprised that the teachings of the evil one are spreading so fast.

<sup>3</sup> My Lord Jesus Christ will hasten his coming, and he will no longer tolerate those who falsify his words.

<sup>4</sup> For I handed on to you at the beginning what I had received from the holy apostles who were before me and who were with Jesus Christ at all times:

<sup>5</sup> that our Lord Jesus Christ was born of Mary, who is of the seed of David according to the flesh[^3-5-1], when the Holy Spirit was sent from heaven by the Father to her through the angel Gabriel,

<sup>6</sup> so that he might come down into this world and redeem all flesh through his flesh, and raise us from the dead in the flesh, as he has shown us in himself, to be our example.

<sup>7</sup> And because humankind was formed by his Father,

<sup>8</sup> it was sought when it was lost, so that it might be made alive by adoption.

<sup>9</sup> For this is why God Almighty, who made heaven and earth, first sent the prophets to the Jews: to turn them away from their sins.

<sup>10</sup> He intended to rescue the house of Israel; so he gave a share of the spirit of Christ to the prophets and sent them first to the Jews, and for a long time they announced the true way to worship God.

<sup>11</sup> But the prince of lawlessness, who wanted to be God, attacked them, put them to death[^3-11-1], and tied all flesh down with evil desires.

<sup>12</sup> But God Almighty, who is just, did not wish to cast aside his own creation, and he took pity on it from heaven,

<sup>13</sup> and he sent his spirit down into Mary, in Galilee,

<sup>15</sup> so that the very flesh through which that wicked one had brought in death[^3-15-1] would be the means of showing him to be defeated.

<sup>16</sup> Through his own body Jesus Christ rescued all flesh,

<sup>17</sup> to display a temple of righteousness in his body,

<sup>18</sup> and by it we are saved.

<sup>19</sup> So whoever sides with them is not a child of righteousness but a child of wrath, rejecting God’s wisdom[^3-19-1] and claiming that the heavens, the earth, and all they hold are not God’s work.

<sup>20</sup> Cursed are they, for they follow the teaching of the serpent.

<sup>21</sup> Drive them out from among you, and keep away from their doctrine.

<sup>24</sup> As for their claim that there is no resurrection of the flesh, they will indeed have no resurrection to life, but to judgment,

<sup>25</sup> because they do not believe in the one who rose from the dead, neither believing nor understanding.

<sup>26</sup> For they do not know, Corinthians, about seeds of wheat[^3-26-1] or of other grains: how they are thrown bare into the ground, decay there, and rise again by God’s will, in bodies and clothed.

<sup>27</sup> And not only the body that is sown rises again, but it is blessed many times over.

<sup>28</sup> And if you want an example not from seeds alone but from nobler bodies,

<sup>29</sup> you know about Jonah[^3-29-1], the son of Amathi: when he would not preach to the people of Nineveh, but fled, he was swallowed by a sea monster.

<sup>30</sup> After three days and three nights God heard Jonah’s prayer from the depths of Hades, and nothing of him was consumed, not even a hair or an eyelash.

<sup>31</sup> How much more, you of little faith, will he raise you who have put your faith in Christ Jesus, just as he himself rose!

<sup>32</sup> In the same way a dead man was thrown by Israelites onto the bones of the prophet Elisha[^3-32-1], and he rose, body and soul, bones and spirit[^3-32-2]. How much more will you, who have been thrown onto the body and bones and spirit of the Lord, rise again on that day with your flesh whole, as he rose?

<sup>34</sup> If you accept any other teaching, God will be witness against you; and let no one trouble me,

<sup>35</sup> for I carry these chains in order to win Christ, and for this reason I bear his marks in my body[^3-35-1], that I may attain the resurrection of the dead.

<sup>36</sup> Whoever holds to the rule received through the blessed prophets and the holy gospel will receive a reward from the Lord.

<sup>37</sup> But whoever departs from these things, the fire is with that person, and with those who walk in the same way.

<sup>38</sup> They are a generation of vipers.

<sup>39</sup> Reject them in the power of the Lord,

<sup>40</sup> and peace, grace, and love will be with you.

# Apocalypse of Peter

A revelation given by Jesus to Peter, written in the first half of the second century, with a vivid tour of the punishments of the wicked and the joys of the righteous. The Muratorian list (around 200) accepts "only the apocalypses of John and Peter," adding that "some of us are not willing that the latter be read in church," and it appears in the Claromontanus list. Clement of Alexandria commented on it as scripture, and in the fifth century the historian Sozomen reports that some churches in Palestine still read it once a year on Good Friday. Parts of the Greek survive in a manuscript found at Akhmim in Egypt and in two small fragments; the full text survives only in Ethiopic.

## Chapter 1

<sup>1</sup> The second coming of Christ and the resurrection of the dead, which Christ revealed to Peter, concerning those who died[^1-1-1] because of their sins, since they did not keep the commandment of God their creator.

<sup>2</sup> Peter[^1-2-1] reflected on this, wanting to grasp the mystery of the Son of God, who is compassionate and delights in showing mercy.

<sup>3</sup> While the Lord was sitting on the Mount of Olives[^1-3-1], his disciples came to him.

<sup>4</sup> We begged him, each of us in turn, and pleaded with him, saying, “Tell us the signs of your coming and of the end of the age[^1-4-1], so that we may know and recognize the time of your coming, and teach those who come after us, to whom we preach the word of your gospel and whom we put in charge in your church, so that when they hear it they may take care and recognize the time of your coming.”

<sup>5</sup> Our Lord answered us, “See to it that no one deceives you, and that you do not fall into doubt and serve other gods. Many will come in my name, saying, ‘I am the Messiah.’[^1-5-1] Do not believe them, and do not go near them. For the coming of the Son of God will not be foreseen[^1-5-2]; it will be like the lightning that shines from the east to the west[^1-5-3]. In that way I will come on the clouds of heaven with a great host, in my majesty, with my cross going ahead of my face. I will come in my majesty, shining seven times brighter than the sun, with all my saints, my angels[^1-5-4]. And my Father will set a crown on my head, so that I may judge the living and the dead and repay everyone according to their works.

## Chapter 2

<sup>1</sup> “Learn a parable from the fig tree[^2-1-1]: as soon as its shoots have come out and its branches have grown, the end of the world will come.”

<sup>2</sup> Then I, Peter, answered him, “Explain the fig tree to me, so that we can understand it. All through its days the fig tree puts out shoots, and every year it yields its fruit for its owner. What, then, does the parable of the fig tree mean? We do not know.”

<sup>3</sup> The Master answered me, “Do you not understand that the fig tree is the house of Israel? It is like a man who planted a fig tree in his garden, and it bore no fruit. He looked for fruit from it for many years, and when he found none he said to the keeper of his garden, ‘Pull up this fig tree, so that it does not make our ground useless.’ The gardener said to the master, ‘Let us clear away the weeds, dig the ground around it, and water it. If it still bears no fruit, we will pull it out by the roots at once and plant another in its place.’ Have you not understood that the fig tree is the house of Israel? Truly I tell you, when its branches have sprouted in the last days, false messiahs[^2-3-1] will come and raise people’s hopes, saying, ‘I am the Messiah, who has now come into the world.’ And when they see the wickedness of what these men do, they will turn away and deny[^2-3-2] the one whom our fathers praised, the first Christ, whom they crucified, and in so doing sinned a great sin. But this deceiver is not the Christ. When they reject him, he will kill them with the sword, and there will be many martyrs. Then the branches of the fig tree, that is, the house of Israel, will put out shoots: many will become martyrs at his hand. Enoch and Elijah will be sent to teach them that this is the deceiver who must come into the world and do signs and wonders in order to deceive. Therefore those who die at his hand will be martyrs, and will be counted among the good and righteous martyrs who pleased God during their lives.”

## Chapter 3

<sup>1</sup> He showed me in his right hand the souls of all people, and on the palm of his right hand the picture of what will be brought to completion on the last day: how the righteous and the sinners will be separated, how those who are upright in heart will fare, and how evildoers will be uprooted for all eternity. We saw the sinners weeping in great distress and sorrow, until everyone who saw it with their own eyes wept, righteous people and angels alike, and he himself as well.

<sup>2</sup> I asked him, “Lord, allow me to repeat your word about the sinners: ‘It would be better for them if they had not been created.’[^3-2-1]” The Savior answered me, “Peter, why do you say that it would have been better for them not to be created? You are resisting God. You would show more compassion than he does for his own image, but he created them and brought them out of non-being. Now, because you have seen the lamentation that will come upon the sinners in the last days, your heart is troubled. But I will show you their works, by which they have sinned against the Most High.

## Chapter 4

<sup>1</sup> “Now look at what will come upon them in the last days, when the day of God comes, the day of decision in God’s judgment. From east to west all the children of humankind will be gathered before my Father, who lives forever. And he will command hell to open its bars of adamant[^4-1-1] and give up everything in it.

<sup>2</sup> “He will command the wild animals and the birds to return all the flesh they have eaten, because he wants people to appear; for nothing is lost in the sight of God, and nothing is impossible for him, since all things are his.

<sup>3</sup> “Everything happens on the day of decision, on the day of judgment, at the word of God. Just as all things were done when he created the world—he gave the command about everything in it, and it was done—so it will be in the last days, for all things are possible with God. That is why he says in scripture, ‘Son of man, prophesy over each of the bones[^4-3-1] and say to the bones: Bone to bone in joints, sinews, nerves, flesh, skin, and hair upon it.’

<sup>4</sup> “And the great Uriel will give them soul and spirit at God’s command, for God has put him in charge of the resurrection of the dead on the day of judgment.

<sup>5</sup> “Think of the grains of wheat[^4-5-1] that are sown in the ground. People sow them in the earth dry and lifeless, and they live again and bear fruit, and the earth gives them back, as something entrusted to its care.

<sup>6</sup> “And what dies[^4-6-1], and is sown in the earth like seed, and comes to life and is restored to life, is a human being.

<sup>7</sup> “How much more will God raise up, on the day of decision, those who believe in him and are chosen by him, for whose sake he made the world! And on the day of decision the earth will give back everything, for it too will be judged along with them, and the heaven with it.

## Chapter 5

<sup>1</sup> “This will come on the day of judgment upon those who have fallen away from faith in God and have sinned. Floods of fire[^5-1-1] will be let loose, and darkness and gloom will rise up and wrap and cover the whole world. The waters will be changed and turned into burning coals, and everything in them will burn, and the sea will become fire. Under the heaven there will be a sharp fire that cannot be put out, and it flows to carry out the judgment of wrath. The stars will be melted by flames of fire, as though they had never been created, and the powers of heaven[^5-1-2] will pass away for lack of water and become as though they had never been. The lightnings of heaven will be no more, and by their sorcery they will terrify the world[^5-1-3]. The spirits of the dead bodies will be like them and will turn to fire at God’s command.

<sup>2</sup> “As soon as the whole creation dissolves, those who are in the east will flee to the west, and those in the west to the east; those in the south will flee to the north, and those in the north to the south. Everywhere the wrath of a terrible fire will overtake them, and an unquenchable flame will drive them and bring them to the judgment of wrath, to the stream of unquenchable fire that flows burning with flames. And when its waves part from one another, burning, there will be great gnashing of teeth among the children of humankind.

## Chapter 6

<sup>1</sup> “Then they will all see me coming on an everlasting cloud of brightness; and the angels of God who are with me will sit[^6-1-1] on the throne of my glory at the right hand of my heavenly Father, and he will place a crown on my head. When the nations see it, they will weep[^6-1-2], each nation by itself.

<sup>2</sup> “Then he will command them to enter the river of fire, while the works of each one stand before them, and everyone will be repaid according to their deeds[^6-2-1]. The elect who have done good will come to me and will not see death by the devouring fire. But the unrighteous, the sinners, and the hypocrites will stand in the depths of a darkness that does not pass away; their punishment is fire, and angels bring forward their sins and prepare a place where they will be punished forever, each one according to their transgression.

<sup>3</sup> “Uriel, the angel of God, will bring out the souls of those sinners who perished in the flood, and of all who lived in all the idols, in every molten image, in every object of devotion, and in paintings, and of those who lived on all the hills, in stones, and beside the roads, whom people called gods. They will be burned along with them[^6-3-1] in everlasting fire; and after all of them and their dwelling places have been destroyed, they will be punished eternally.

## Chapter 7

<sup>1</sup> “Then men and women will come[^7-1-1] to the place prepared for them. They will be hung up by their tongues, with which they blasphemed the way of righteousness. A fire that cannot be put out is spread beneath them, so that they cannot escape it.

<sup>2</sup> “Look, another place: a great pit, full[^7-2-1]. In it are those who have denied righteousness, and angels of punishment torment them and kindle the fire of their torment upon them.

<sup>3</sup> “And look again: two women[^7-3-1] are hung up by their necks and their hair, and they will be thrown into the pit. These are the women who braided their hair, not to make themselves beautiful but to lead people into sexual immorality, to trap their souls in ruin. The men who lay with them in sexual immorality will be hung by their loins in that place of fire, and they will say to one another, ‘We did not know that we would come into everlasting punishment.’

<sup>4</sup> “The murderers, and those who made common cause with them, they will throw into the fire, in a place full of poisonous beasts, and they will be tormented without rest, feeling their pains, and their worms will be as many as a dark cloud. The angel Ezrael[^7-4-1] will bring out the souls of those who were killed, and they will watch the torment of those who killed them and say to one another, ‘Righteous and just is the judgment of God[^7-4-2]. For we heard, but we did not believe, that we would come to this place of eternal judgment.’

## Chapter 8

<sup>1</sup> “Near this flame there is a pit[^8-1-1], great and very deep, and into it flow from above all kinds of torment, filth, and excrement. Women are sunk in it up to their necks and tormented with great pain. These are the women who caused their children to be born too soon and so ruined the work of God who made them. Opposite them will be another place where their children sit alive and cry out to God. Flashes of lightning come from those children and pierce the eyes of those who, because of sexual immorality, caused their destruction.

<sup>2</sup> “Other men and women will stand above them, naked, while their children stand opposite them in a place of delight, sighing and crying out to God because of their parents: ‘These are the ones who despised and cursed and broke your commandments and handed us over to death. They cursed the angel who formed us, they hung us up[^8-2-1], and they withheld from us the light that you have given to all creatures.’ The milk of their mothers, flowing from their breasts, will congeal, and from it will come flesh-eating beasts, which will come out and turn and torment them forever, together with their husbands, because they abandoned the commandments of God and killed their children. Their children will be handed over to the angel Temlakos[^8-2-2]. And those who killed them will be tormented eternally, for God wills it so.

## Chapter 9

<sup>1</sup> “Ezrael, the angel of wrath, will bring men and women with half of their bodies burning and throw them into a place of darkness, the hell of men. A spirit of wrath will punish them with every kind of torment, and a worm that never sleeps will eat their entrails. These are the persecutors and betrayers[^9-1-1] of my righteous ones.

<sup>2</sup> “Beside them will be other men and women gnawing their tongues[^9-2-1], and they will torment them with red-hot irons and burn their eyes. These are the ones who slander and doubt my righteousness.

<sup>3</sup> “Other men and women, whose works were done in deceit, will have their lips cut off[^9-3-1], and fire enters their mouths and their entrails. These are the ones who caused the martyrs to die by their lies.

<sup>4</sup> “Near them, on the rock, will be a pillar of fire[^9-4-1], and the pillar is sharper than swords. Men and women dressed in rags and filthy clothes will be thrown onto it to suffer the judgment of endless torment. These are the ones who trusted in their riches and despised widows and women with fatherless children ... before God[^9-4-2].

## Chapter 10

<sup>1</sup> “In another place nearby, full of filth, they throw men and women in up to their knees. These are the ones who lent money and charged interest[^10-1-1].

<sup>2</sup> “Other men and women hurl themselves down from a high place, then get up and run again, and demons drive them. These are the idol worshipers. The demons drive them up to the top of the height, and they hurl themselves down, and they do this continually and are tormented forever. These are the ones who have cut their flesh as ... of a man[^10-2-1]; and the women with them ...; and these are the men who defiled themselves together like women[^10-2-2].

<sup>3</sup> “Beside them ...[^10-3-1] and below them the angel Ezrael will prepare a place of great fire. All the idols of gold and silver, all idols, the work of human hands, and the likenesses of cats and lions, of crawling things and wild animals, and the men and women who made the images, will be in chains of fire and will be punished for their error before the idols; and this is their judgment forever.

<sup>4</sup> “And beside them will be other men and women burning in the fire of judgment, and their torment is everlasting. These are the ones who abandoned the commandment of God[^10-4-2] and followed the persuasions[^10-4-1] of demons.

## Chapter 11

<sup>1</sup> “There will be another place, very high ...[^11-1-1] The men and women whose feet slip will roll down into a place of fear. And while the fire that has been prepared flows, they climb up and fall down again and keep rolling down. In this way they will be tormented forever. These are the ones who did not honor their father and mother, and of their own accord kept away from them. Therefore they will be punished eternally.

<sup>2</sup> “Furthermore, the angel Ezrael will bring children and young women to show them those who are tormented. They will be punished with pains, with hanging up[^11-2-1], and with a multitude of wounds that flesh-eating birds will inflict on them. These are the ones who put their trust in their sins, who do not obey their parents, do not follow the instruction of their fathers, and do not honor those older than they are.

<sup>3</sup> “Beside them will be girls clothed in darkness, and they will be severely punished, and their flesh will be torn to pieces. These are the ones who did not keep their virginity until they were given in marriage, and they will be punished with these torments and will feel them.

<sup>4</sup> “And again, other men and women, gnawing their tongues without ceasing and tormented in everlasting fire. These are the slaves[^11-4-1] who did not obey their masters, and this is their judgment forever.

## Chapter 12

<sup>1</sup> “Near this place of torment there will be men and women who are mute and blind, dressed in white. They will crowd against one another and fall onto coals of fire that cannot be put out. These are the ones who give alms and say, ‘We are righteous before God,’ though they have not sought righteousness.

<sup>2</sup> “Ezrael, the angel of God, will bring them out of this fire and set up a decisive judgment[^12-2-1]. This is their judgment. A river of fire will flow, and all who are judged will be dragged down into the middle of the river. And Uriel will place them there.

<sup>3</sup> “And there are wheels of fire, and men and women hung on them by the force of the spinning. Those in the pit will burn; these are the sorcerers and sorceresses. Those wheels will be in every judgment by fire, without number[^12-3-1].

## Chapter 13

<sup>1</sup> “After that the angels will bring my elect and righteous ones, who are perfect in all uprightness, and carry them in their hands, and clothe them in the garment of the life above. They will see their desire fulfilled on those who hated them[^13-1-1], when he punishes them, and each one’s torment will last forever, according to their works.

<sup>2</sup> “All those in torment will say with one voice, ‘Have mercy on us, for now we know the judgment of God, which he declared to us beforehand, and we did not believe.’ Then the angel Tatirokos[^13-2-1] will come and punish them with even greater torment, and say to them, ‘Now you repent, when it is no longer time for repentance and nothing of life is left.’ And they will say, ‘Righteous is the judgment of God[^13-2-2], for we have heard and understood that his judgment is good, since we are repaid according to our deeds.’

## Chapter 14

<sup>1</sup> “Then I will give my elect and righteous ones the baptism[^14-1-1] and the salvation that they asked me for, in the field called Akrosja[^14-1-2], which is named Aneslasleja[^14-1-3]. They will adorn with flowers the portion of the righteous, and I will go ... I will rejoice with them. I will bring the nations into my everlasting kingdom and show them the eternal good things on which I have made them set their hope, I and my Father in heaven.

<sup>2</sup> “I have told you this, Peter, and explained it to you. Go, then, to the city of the west, and enter the vineyard[^14-2-1] that I will show you, so that through the sufferings of the Son, who is without sin, the works of corruption may be made holy. As for you, you are chosen according to the promise I gave you. Spread my gospel in peace throughout the whole world. Truly, people will rejoice: my words will be the source of hope and of life, and suddenly the world will be carried off.”

## Chapter 15

<sup>1</sup> My Lord Jesus Christ, our king, said to me, “Let us go to the holy mountain[^15-1-1].” And his disciples went with him[^15-1-2], praying.

<sup>2</sup> And there were two men there[^15-2-1], and we could not look at their faces, for a light came from them, shining brighter than the sun, and their clothing also shone and cannot be described; nothing in this world is enough to compare with it. And the sweetness of them ... no mouth can express the beauty of their appearance, for they looked astonishing and wonderful. And the other, great, I say, shines in his appearance above crystal. The color of his appearance is like the flower of roses, and of his body ... his head. And upon his shoulders ... and on their foreheads was a crown of nard woven from beautiful flowers. Their hair was like the rainbow in the water[^15-2-2]. Such was the beauty of their faces, adorned with every kind of ornament.

## Chapter 16

<sup>1</sup> When we suddenly saw them, we marveled. I went up to God, Jesus Christ, and said to him, “My Lord, who are these?” He said to me, “They are Moses and Elijah[^16-1-1].” I said to him, “Where, then, are Abraham, Isaac, Jacob, and the other righteous fathers?” And he showed us a great garden, open[^16-1-2], full of beautiful trees and blessed fruits and the fragrance of perfumes. The scent was sweet and reached us. And from that tree ... I saw much fruit. And my Lord and God Jesus Christ said to me, “Have you seen the companies of the fathers?”

<sup>2</sup> “As their rest is, so also are the honor and the glory of those who are persecuted for the sake of my righteousness[^16-2-1].” I rejoiced and believed and understood what is written in the book of my Lord Jesus Christ. And I said to him, “My Lord, do you want me to make three shelters here[^16-2-2], one for you, one for Moses, and one for Elijah?” He said to me in anger, “Satan is making war against you and has veiled your understanding, and the good things of this world are overpowering you. Your eyes must be opened and your ears unstopped, so that you may see a shelter not made by human hands, which my heavenly Father has made for me and for the elect.” And we saw it and were filled with joy.

## Chapter 17

<sup>1</sup> And suddenly a voice came from heaven, saying, “This is my beloved Son, in whom I am well pleased[^17-1-1]; he has kept my commandments.” Then a great and exceedingly white cloud came over our heads and carried away our Lord, Moses, and Elijah. I trembled and was afraid. We looked up, and the heaven opened, and we saw people in the flesh. They came and greeted our Lord, Moses, and Elijah, and went into another heaven. And the word of scripture was fulfilled: “This is the generation of those who seek him[^17-1-2], who seek the face of the God of Jacob.” Great fear and commotion arose in heaven, and the angels pressed against one another so that the word of scripture might be fulfilled, which says, “Lift up your gates, you princes[^17-1-3].”

<sup>2</sup> After this, the heaven that had been open was shut.

<sup>3</sup> And we prayed and came down from the mountain, glorifying God, who has written the names of the righteous in heaven in the book of life[^17-3-1].

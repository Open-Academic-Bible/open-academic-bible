# Ignatius to the Romans

Chapters: [1](#chapter-1) · [2](#chapter-2) · [3](#chapter-3) · [4](#chapter-4) · [5](#chapter-5) · [6](#chapter-6) · [7](#chapter-7) · [8](#chapter-8) · [9](#chapter-9) · [10](#chapter-10)

<sup>1</sup> From Ignatius, also called Theophorus[^0-1-1], to her who received mercy in the majesty of the Most High Father and of Jesus Christ, his only Son. She is the church loved and illuminated by the will of the one who willed all things that are, in keeping with the love of Jesus Christ our God, and she sits at the head in the place of the Roman territory: worthy of God, worthy of honor, worthy of congratulation, worthy of praise, worthy of success, worthy of purity, first in love, named for Christ and for the Father. I salute her in the name of Jesus Christ, the Father’s Son, and I salute those who are bound together, in flesh and spirit, by his every commandment, filled to the brim with God’s grace and filtered of every stranger’s tint. Joy to you, abundantly, in Jesus Christ our God, and without blame.

## Chapter 1

<sup>1</sup> By praying to God I have been allowed to see your godly faces, and I have received even more than I asked. For it is in chains in Christ Jesus that I hope to greet you, if it is his will that I be counted worthy to the end.

<sup>2</sup> The beginning is well arranged, provided that I receive the favor of claiming my allotted share without hindrance. But I fear your love, in case it does me harm. For you it is easy to do what you wish; for me it is hard to reach God, if you spare me[^1-2-1].

## Chapter 2

<sup>1</sup> My wish is not that you please people, but that you please God, as indeed you do. I will never have another chance like this to reach God; and you, if you keep quiet, cannot have your name written on any finer deed. If you are silent about me, I am God’s word. If you fall in love with my flesh, I will once more be nothing but a voice.

<sup>2</sup> Do not give me more than this: to be poured out for God[^2-2-1] while there is still an altar made ready. Then you can gather as a choir in love and sing to the Father in Christ Jesus, that God has judged the bishop of Syria fit to be found in the west, summoning him out of the east. How good to sink out of the world toward God, so that I may rise in him.

## Chapter 3

<sup>1</sup> You have never envied anyone; you have taught others. But I want what you command when you make disciples to remain firm.

<sup>2</sup> Only ask power for me, inside and outside, so that I may not merely speak but also want, so that I may not merely be called a Christian but also be found one. For if I am found one, I can also be called one, and be faithful then, when I no longer appear to the world.

<sup>3</sup> No visible thing is good. Our God Jesus Christ, now in the Father, is seen all the more. Christianity is not a task of persuasion but of greatness, whenever the world hates it.

## Chapter 4

<sup>1</sup> I am writing to every church, and I am commanding everyone, that I die for God of my own free will, unless you prevent it. I appeal to you: do not do me a kindness at the wrong time. Leave me to be eaten by the wild beasts; through them I can get to God. I am God’s grain, and the teeth of animals will crush me so that I can be found as Christ’s pure bread.

<sup>2</sup> Better still, make the animals gentle toward me, so that they will be my grave, and no part of my body is left behind and nobody has the burden of my burial once I have gone to sleep. Then I will be a real disciple of Jesus Christ, when the world cannot even see my body. Beg Christ for me, so that by means of these instruments I will be found to be a sacrifice.

<sup>3</sup> I am not commanding you the way Peter and Paul did. They were apostles, and I am a convict; they were free, and I am a slave to this day. But if I suffer, I will become Jesus Christ’s freed person[^4-3-1] and will rise free in him. For now, bound, I learn to want nothing.

## Chapter 5

<sup>1</sup> From Syria to Rome I am fighting wild animals, by land and sea, night and day, bound to ten leopards, that is, a squad of soldiers, who become worse when they are treated well. Yet through their wrongs I become more of a disciple, though I am not justified by this[^5-1-1].

<sup>2</sup> I hope to profit from the animals that are made ready for me. I pray they will be found quick; I will even coax them to devour me at once, unlike those whom they feared and did not touch. And if they refuse of their own will, I will force them.

<sup>3</sup> Bear with me. I know what is good for me. Now is when I begin to be a disciple. May nothing seen or unseen grudge me my reaching Jesus Christ. Fire and cross, gatherings of beasts, cutting, tearing, scattering of bones, chopping up of limbs, crushing of the entire body, the devil’s evil torments: let them all come at me, if only I reach Jesus Christ.

## Chapter 6

<sup>1</sup> The farthest regions of the world and the kingdoms of this age are worth nothing to me. For me, dying into Christ Jesus is better than reigning over the ends of the earth. I am looking for the one who died for us; I want the one who rose because of us. The labor pains are close.

<sup>2</sup> Pardon me, brothers and sisters[^6-2-1]. Do not stand in the way of my living; do not choose death for me. Do not give to the world a person who longs to be God’s, and do not mislead him with matter. Let me take hold of the pure light. Once I arrive there, I will be fully human.

<sup>3</sup> Let me be an imitator of my God’s passion. If anyone has him inside, let that person grasp what I long for and feel with me, because they know what presses on me.

## Chapter 7

<sup>1</sup> The ruler of this age is eager to carry me off and wreck my resolve toward my God. So none of you who are there should assist him. Take my side, rather, which is God’s. Do not speak of Jesus Christ and yet crave the world.

<sup>2</sup> Let no envy live among you. Even if I myself, when I am present, urge you, do not be persuaded by me; be persuaded by what I write to you. I am alive as I write to you, in love with dying. My desire has been crucified[^7-2-1], and there is in me no fire that loves matter; but there is living water[^7-2-2], speaking in me, saying within: “Come to the Father.”

<sup>3</sup> I take no pleasure in perishable food or the pleasures of this life. I want God’s bread[^7-3-1], which is the flesh of Jesus Christ, from the seed of David[^7-3-2], and for drink I want his blood, which is imperishable love.

## Chapter 8

<sup>1</sup> I no longer want to live by human standards, and this will be so if you want it. Want it, so that you also may be wanted.

<sup>2</sup> I ask you in a few lines: believe me. Jesus Christ will make it clear to you that I speak the truth; he is the mouth that cannot lie, by which the Father has truly spoken.

<sup>3</sup> Ask for me, that I may reach it. I wrote to you not by human reasoning but according to the mind of God. If I suffer, you wanted it; if I am rejected, you hated me.

## Chapter 9

<sup>1</sup> Remember in your prayers the church in Syria, which has God for a shepherd instead of me. Only Jesus Christ will watch over it, he and your love.

<sup>2</sup> I am embarrassed to be named among them, since I am not worthy to be, being the last of them and one born at the wrong time[^9-2-1]. But I have been shown mercy, to be somebody, if I reach God.

<sup>3</sup> My spirit greets you, and so does the love of the churches that received me in the name of Jesus Christ, not as a passerby. Even those that were not on my route in the flesh went ahead of me, city by city.

## Chapter 10

<sup>1</sup> I am writing you this from Smyrna by way of the Ephesians, who are worthy of every blessing. With me, along with many others, is also Crocus, a name dear to me.

<sup>2</sup> Concerning those who went ahead of me from Syria to Rome for the glory of God, I trust you have learned of them. Tell them that I am near. They are all worthy of God and of you, and it is right for you to refresh them in every way.

<sup>3</sup> I wrote you all this on the ninth day before the Kalends of September[^10-3-1]. Farewell until the end, in the perseverance of Jesus Christ.

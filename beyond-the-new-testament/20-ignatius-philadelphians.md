# Ignatius to the Philadelphians

Chapters: [1](#chapter-1) · [2](#chapter-2) · [3](#chapter-3) · [4](#chapter-4) · [5](#chapter-5) · [6](#chapter-6) · [7](#chapter-7) · [8](#chapter-8) · [9](#chapter-9) · [10](#chapter-10) · [11](#chapter-11)

<sup>1</sup> Ignatius, who is also called Theophorus[^0-1-1], to the church of God the Father and of the Lord Jesus Christ in Philadelphia of Asia: a church that has found mercy, been firmly set in God’s harmony, exulted without doubt in our Lord’s suffering, and been convinced of his resurrection with all mercy. I greet it in the blood of Jesus Christ, which is eternal, lasting joy, above all when its people are united with the bishop and with the presbyters and deacons alongside him, who were appointed according to the mind of Jesus Christ and whom he strengthened, by his own will, with certainty through his Holy Spirit.

## Chapter 1

<sup>1</sup> I recognize that this bishop obtained the ministry that serves the whole community not from himself, nor through human beings, nor out of empty pride, but in the love of God the Father and the Lord Jesus Christ. I am amazed at his gentleness: in silence he can accomplish more than those who speak empty words.

<sup>2</sup> He is in tune with the commandments like strings on a lyre. That is why my soul counts his mind toward God blessed, since I recognize it to be virtuous and perfect, as well as his steadiness and freedom from anger, in all the gentleness of the living God.

## Chapter 2

<sup>1</sup> Children of the light[^2-1-1] of truth, then, flee division and bad teaching. Where the shepherd is, follow there like sheep.

<sup>2</sup> Many wolves[^2-2-1] who seem trustworthy take captive, with a wicked pleasure, those who run God’s race; but in your unity they will find no place.

## Chapter 3

<sup>1</sup> Keep away from the harmful weeds, which Jesus Christ does not cultivate, because they are not the Father’s planting[^3-1-1]. It is not that I found division among you, but a filtering out.

<sup>2</sup> All who belong to God and Jesus Christ are with the bishop. And all who repent and come to the unity of the church will also belong to God, living in accord with Jesus Christ.

<sup>3</sup> Do not be led astray, my brothers and sisters[^3-3-1]: anyone who follows someone who causes a split will not inherit God’s kingdom[^3-3-2], and anyone who walks in a different opinion[^3-3-3] does not share in the passion.

## Chapter 4

<sup>1</sup> Be eager, then, to hold one Eucharist. There is one flesh of our Lord Jesus Christ, and one cup to unite us in his blood, one altar, just as there is one bishop together with the presbytery and the deacons, my fellow slaves, so that whatever you do, you do it according to God.

## Chapter 5

<sup>1</sup> My brothers and sisters[^5-1-1], my love for you has been poured out beyond measure, and in my overflowing joy I am watching over your safety, though it is not I but Jesus Christ, in whose cause I am chained and so fear all the more, since I am not yet fully equipped[^5-1-2]. Still, your prayer to God will equip me to gain the lot I was mercifully given, as I flee for refuge to the gospel as if to Jesus’ flesh, and to the apostles as if to the church’s body of elders.

<sup>2</sup> The prophets we also love, because they too proclaimed the good news, put their hope in him, and waited for him. It was by trusting in him that they were saved: they stand in the unity of Jesus Christ, saints to be loved and admired, vouched for by Jesus Christ and numbered with us in the gospel of our shared hope.

## Chapter 6

<sup>1</sup> Should anyone expound Judaism to you, pay no attention. It is better to hear Christianity from someone who is circumcised than Judaism from someone who is not. And if neither of them talks about Jesus Christ, they are to me nothing but memorial pillars and tombstones, on which just the names of human beings are inscribed.

<sup>2</sup> Flee, then, the evil tricks and traps of the ruler of this age, so that you will not be weakened in love by being squeezed by his opinion. Instead, all of you come together with an undivided heart.

<sup>3</sup> I give thanks to my God that I have a clear conscience concerning you, and nobody can say, secretly or openly, that I weighed on them in a small matter or a large one. And for all to whom I have spoken, I pray that they will not end up with it as evidence against them.

## Chapter 7

<sup>1</sup> Even if certain people wanted to deceive me by human means, the Spirit is not deceived, because it comes from God. It knows where it has come from[^7-1-1] and where it goes, and it exposes what is secret. I called out while I was in your midst, speaking with a great voice, the voice of God: “Give heed to the bishop, the presbytery, and the deacons.”

<sup>2</sup> Some suspected that I said this because I had foreknowledge of a division among certain people. But the one for whom I wear chains is my witness that I learned nothing from a human being. It was the Spirit who proclaimed, saying: “Do nothing without the bishop; guard your flesh as the shrine of God[^7-2-1]; cherish union; run away from divisions; follow the example of Jesus Christ[^7-2-2], as he followed his Father’s.”

## Chapter 8

<sup>1</sup> I, for my part, was doing what was my own business, as a person fitted for unity. Where there is division and rage, God has no home. The Lord pardons all who repent, provided their repentance leads to God’s unity and to the bishop’s council. I believe in the grace of Jesus Christ, who will free you from every bond.

<sup>2</sup> I urge you to act from no spirit of rivalry, but from what you have learned of Christ. I once heard people saying that unless they find it in the archives[^8-2-1], in the gospel, they do not believe it. When I answered, “It stands written,” they retorted, “That is exactly what has to be shown.” To my mind, Jesus Christ is the archives. The untouchable archives are his cross and death and rising, and the faith that comes by him; by these, through your prayers, I wish to be made righteous[^8-2-2].

## Chapter 9

<sup>1</sup> Priests are good, but better is the high priest who has been entrusted with the holy of holies, who alone has been entrusted with the secrets of God. He is the door of the Father[^9-1-1], through which Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob, the prophets, the apostles, and the church enter. All of this is for the unity of God.

<sup>2</sup> But the gospel has something distinctive: the coming of the Savior, our Lord Jesus Christ, his suffering, and his resurrection. The beloved prophets made their announcement looking toward him, but the gospel is the completion of incorruption. All things together are good, if you believe in love.

## Chapter 10

<sup>1</sup> Because I was told that the church in Antioch in Syria is at peace, a result of your prayer and the tender care you have in Christ Jesus, it would be right for you, as God’s church, to elect a deacon to serve there as God’s envoy, to share their joy now that they are together again and to glorify the name.

<sup>2</sup> Blessed in Jesus Christ is whoever is counted worthy of such a service, and you will be glorified. If you are willing, it is not impossible for you, for the name of God, as the nearest churches have also sent bishops, and others presbyters and deacons.

## Chapter 11

<sup>1</sup> As for Philo, the deacon from Cilicia, a man of proven character, who is even now assisting me in the word of God together with Rheus Agathopus, an outstanding man who has been following me from Syria and has bid life farewell: these two also testify on your behalf, and I thank God for you, that you received them as the Lord received you. As for those who treated them without honor, may they be set free by the grace of Jesus Christ.

<sup>2</sup> Greetings to you from the love of the brothers and sisters[^11-2-1] in Troas. I write to you from there through Burrhus, who was sent along with me by the Ephesians and Smyrnaeans as a token of respect. May the Lord Jesus Christ honor them. They hope in him with body, soul, spirit, faith, love, and harmony. Farewell in Christ Jesus, our shared hope.

# 2 Thessalonians

Chapters: [1](#chapter-1) · [2](#chapter-2) · [3](#chapter-3)

## Chapter 1

<sup>1</sup> Paul, Silvanus, and Timothy, To the church of the Thessalonians in God our Father and the Lord Jesus Christ:

<sup>2</sup> Grace and peace to you from God the Father[^1-2-1] and the Lord Jesus Christ.

<sup>3</sup> We are obligated to thank God for you all the time, brothers and sisters, as is fitting, because your faith grows exceedingly, and the love each of you has for one another keeps increasing.

<sup>4</sup> That is why we boast among God’s churches about your perseverance and faith in the face of all the persecution and affliction you are enduring.

<sup>5</sup> All this is evidence of God’s righteous judgment, so that you may be made worthy of the kingdom of God, for which you are suffering.

<sup>6</sup> After all, it is only right for God to repay with affliction those who afflict you,

<sup>7</sup> and to grant relief to you who are afflicted and to us as well. This will take place when the Lord Jesus is revealed from heaven with his mighty angels

<sup>8</sup> in flaming fire, exacting vengeance on those who do not know God and on those who do not obey the gospel of our Lord Jesus.

<sup>9</sup> They will suffer the penalty of eternal destruction, separated from the presence of the Lord and the glory of his might,

<sup>10</sup> on the day he comes to be glorified by his saints and regarded with wonder among all those who have believed, because the testimony we gave you was believed.

<sup>11</sup> To this end, we always pray for you, that our God would make you worthy of his calling and by his power bring to fulfillment every resolve for good and every work of faith,

<sup>12</sup> so that the name of our Lord Jesus will be glorified in you, and you in him, according to the grace of our God and of the Lord Jesus Christ.

## Chapter 2

<sup>1</sup> Now concerning the coming of our Lord Jesus Christ and our being gathered together to him, we ask you, brothers and sisters,

<sup>2</sup> not to be easily disconcerted or alarmed by any spirit or message or letter seeming to be from us, alleging that the day of the Lord has already come.

<sup>3</sup> Let no one deceive you in any way, for it will not come unless first the rebellion comes and the lawless one[^2-3-1], the one bound for destruction[^2-3-2], is revealed.

<sup>4</sup> He is the one who opposes and exalts himself above everything called a god or an object of worship, to the point of taking his seat in the temple of God and proclaiming himself to be God.

<sup>5</sup> Do you not remember that I told you these things while I was still with you?

<sup>6</sup> And you know what is now restraining him, so that he may be revealed at the proper time.

<sup>7</sup> For the mystery of lawlessness is already at work, but the one who now restrains it will continue until he is taken out of the way.

<sup>8</sup> And then the lawless one will be revealed, whom the Lord Jesus[^2-8-1] will slay[^2-8-2] with the breath of his mouth and annihilate by the appearing of his coming.

<sup>9</sup> The coming of the lawless one is marked by the working of Satan, with all power, signs, and lying wonders,

<sup>10</sup> and with every wicked deception for those who are perishing, because they would not love the truth that they might be saved.

<sup>11</sup> For this reason God is sending them a powerful delusion so that they believe what is false,

<sup>12</sup> in order that all who have disbelieved the truth and delighted in wickedness may be condemned.

<sup>13</sup> But we should always thank God for you, brothers and sisters who are loved by the Lord, because God chose you as firstfruits[^2-13-1] to be saved by the sanctification of the Spirit and by faith in the truth.

<sup>14</sup> He called you to this through our gospel, so that you may gain the glory that belongs to our Lord Jesus Christ.

<sup>15</sup> Therefore, stand firm, brothers and sisters, and cling to the traditions we taught you, whether by speech or by letter.

<sup>16</sup> Now may our Lord Jesus Christ himself and God our Father, who by grace has loved us and given us eternal comfort and good hope,

<sup>17</sup> encourage your hearts and strengthen you in every good word and deed.

## Chapter 3

<sup>1</sup> Finally, pray for us, brothers and sisters, that the word of the Lord may spread quickly and be glorified, just as it is with you.

<sup>2</sup> And pray that we may be delivered from wicked and evil persons; for not everyone has faith.

<sup>3</sup> But the Lord is faithful, and he will strengthen you and guard you from the evil one[^3-3-1].

<sup>4</sup> And we have confidence in the Lord that you are doing and will continue to do what we command.

<sup>5</sup> May the Lord direct your hearts into God’s love and Christ’s perseverance.

<sup>6</sup> Now we command you in the name of our Lord Jesus Christ, brothers and sisters, to keep away from any brother or sister who lives irresponsibly, not in keeping with the tradition they received from us[^3-6-1].

<sup>7</sup> For you yourselves know how you ought to imitate us, because we did not live irresponsibly among you,

<sup>8</sup> nor did we eat anyone’s food without paying for it. Instead, in labor and toil, we worked night and day so that we would not be a burden to any of you.

<sup>9</sup> Not that we lack this right, but we wanted to offer ourselves as an example for you to imitate.

<sup>10</sup> For even while we were with you, we gave you this command: “If anyone is unwilling to work, that person is not to eat.”

<sup>11</sup> For we hear that some of you are living irresponsibly, accomplishing nothing, but being busybodies.

<sup>12</sup> In the Lord Jesus Christ we command and urge such people to begin working quietly and support themselves.

<sup>13</sup> But as for you, do not grow weary in well-doing, brothers and sisters.

<sup>14</sup> Take note of anyone who does not obey the instructions we have given in this letter. Do not associate with such a person, so that he or she may be ashamed.

<sup>15</sup> Yet do not regard that person as an enemy, but admonish him or her as a brother or sister.

<sup>16</sup> Now may the Lord of peace himself give you peace at all times and in every way. The Lord be with all of you.

<sup>17</sup> This greeting is in my own hand—Paul. This is my mark in every letter; it is the way I write.

<sup>18</sup> The grace of our Lord Jesus Christ be with all of you.[^3-18-1]

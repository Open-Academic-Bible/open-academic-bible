# 3 John

## Chapter 1

<sup>1</sup> From the elder, to the beloved Gaius, whom I love in truth:

<sup>2</sup> Beloved, I pray that in every way things may go well for you, and that you may be in good health, as your soul is doing well.

<sup>3</sup> For I was overjoyed when brothers and sisters came and testified about your devotion to the truth, in which you continue to walk.

<sup>4</sup> I have no greater joy than to hear that my children are walking in the truth.

<sup>5</sup> Beloved, you are acting faithfully in all that you do for the brothers and sisters, strangers though they are to you.

<sup>6</sup> They have testified to the church about your love. You will do well to send them on their way in a manner worthy of God.

<sup>7</sup> For they set out for the sake of Christ[^1-7-1], accepting nothing from those who are not believers[^1-7-2].

<sup>8</sup> We therefore ought to support people like these, so that we too become fellow workers with the truth.

<sup>9</sup> I wrote something to the church, but Diotrephes, who loves to be first, does not welcome us.

<sup>10</sup> So if I come, I will bring up what he is doing, spreading malicious slander about us. And not satisfied with that, he refuses to receive the brothers and sisters himself, hinders those who want to, and even puts them out of the church.

<sup>11</sup> Beloved, do not imitate what is evil, but what is good. The one who does good is of God; the one who does evil has not seen God.

<sup>12</sup> Demetrius has received a good testimony from everyone, and from the truth itself[^1-12-1]. We also testify for him[^1-12-2], and you know that our testimony is true.

<sup>13</sup> I have many things to write to you, but I would prefer not to do so with pen and ink.

<sup>14</sup> Instead, I hope to see you soon and speak with you face to face. Peace be with you. The friends send their greetings. Greet each friend there by name.

---

[^1-7-1]: **1:7** Gk for the sake of the name
[^1-7-2]: **1:7** Gk the gentiles
[^1-12-1]: **1:12** Some manuscripts read church or church and truth
[^1-12-2]: **1:12** Gk lacks for him

# Philemon

## Chapter 1

<sup>1</sup> Paul, a prisoner of Christ Jesus, and Timothy our brother, To Philemon our beloved fellow worker,

<sup>2</sup> to Apphia our sister,[^1-2-1] to Archippus our fellow soldier, and to the church that meets at your house:[^1-2-2]

<sup>3</sup> Grace and peace to you from God our Father and the Lord Jesus Christ.

<sup>4</sup> I always thank my God,[^1-4-1] remembering you in my prayers,

<sup>5</sup> because I hear about your faith in the Lord Jesus and your love for all the saints.

<sup>6</sup> I pray that the partnership that springs from your faith will prove effective as you come to understand fully all the good we share in Christ.[^1-6-1][^1-6-2]

<sup>7</sup> I take great joy[^1-7-1] and encouragement in your love, because through you, brother, the saints have had their hearts refreshed.

<sup>8</sup> So although in Christ I am bold enough to order you to do what is proper,

<sup>9</sup> I prefer to appeal on the basis of love. For I, Paul, am now aged, and a prisoner of Christ Jesus as well[^1-9-1].

<sup>10</sup> I appeal to you for my child Onesimus, whose father I became while I was in chains.

<sup>11</sup> He was once of no use to you, but now he has proved useful[^1-11-1] to me and to you.[^1-11-2]

<sup>12</sup> I am sending back to you him who is my very heart.[^1-12-1]

<sup>13</sup> I would have liked to keep him with me, so that on your behalf he could minister to me in my chains for the gospel.

<sup>14</sup> But I did not want to do anything without your consent, so that your goodness will not be out of compulsion, but by your own free will.

<sup>15</sup> For perhaps this is why he was separated from you for a while, so that you might have him back for good—

<sup>16</sup> not as a slave any longer but as more than a slave, as a beloved brother. He is especially beloved to me, but even more so to you, both in person and in the Lord.

<sup>17</sup> So if you consider me a partner, receive him as you would receive me.

<sup>18</sup> But if he has wronged you in any way or owes you anything, charge it to my account.

<sup>19</sup> I, Paul, write this with my own hand. I will repay it—not to mention that you owe me your very self.

<sup>20</sup> Yes, brother, let me have some benefit from you in the Lord. Refresh my heart in Christ[^1-20-1].

<sup>21</sup> Confident of your obedience, I write to you, knowing that you will do even more than I ask.

<sup>22</sup> In the meantime, prepare a guest room for me, because I hope that through your prayers I will be restored to you.

<sup>23</sup> Epaphras, my fellow prisoner in Christ Jesus, sends you greetings,[^1-23-1]

<sup>24</sup> as do Mark, Aristarchus, Demas, and Luke, my fellow workers.

<sup>25</sup> The grace of the Lord Jesus Christ be with your spirit.[^1-25-1]

---

[^1-2-1]: **1:2** Some manuscripts read beloved
[^1-2-2]: **1:2** Gk your is singular
[^1-4-1]: **1:4** Gk you is singular in verses 4–21
[^1-6-1]: **1:6** Some manuscripts read you (plural)
[^1-6-2]: **1:6** Some manuscripts add Jesus
[^1-7-1]: **1:7** Some manuscripts read we have
[^1-9-1]: **1:9** Or an ambassador, and now also a prisoner of Christ Jesus
[^1-11-1]: **1:11** Gk the name Onesimus means useful
[^1-11-2]: **1:11** Some manuscripts read both to
[^1-12-1]: **1:12** Some manuscripts read you; receive him
[^1-20-1]: **1:20** Some manuscripts read in the Lord
[^1-23-1]: **1:23** Gk you is singular here
[^1-25-1]: **1:25** Some manuscripts add Amen

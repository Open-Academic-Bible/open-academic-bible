# 2 John

## Chapter 1

<sup>1</sup> The elder, To the chosen lady and her children, whom I love in the truth—and not I alone, but also all who know the truth—

<sup>2</sup> because of the truth that abides in us and will be with us forever:

<sup>3</sup> Grace, mercy, and peace from God the Father and from[^1-3-1] Jesus Christ, the Son of the Father, will be with us in truth and love.

<sup>4</sup> I was overjoyed to find some of your children walking in the truth, just as the Father has commanded us.

<sup>5</sup> And now I urge you, dear lady—not as a new commandment to you, but one we have had from the beginning—that we love one another.

<sup>6</sup> Love consists in this: that we walk according to his commandments. And this is the commandment you have heard from the beginning: walk in it.

<sup>7</sup> For deceivers in large numbers have gone out into the world—people who will not acknowledge Jesus Christ as having come in the flesh. That kind of person is the deceiver and the antichrist.

<sup>8</sup> Watch yourselves, so that you do not lose what we[^1-8-1] have worked for, but that you may be fully rewarded.

<sup>9</sup> Everyone who runs ahead[^1-9-1] and does not remain in the teaching of Christ does not have God. The one who remains in the teaching[^1-9-2] has the Father as well as the Son.

<sup>10</sup> Should anyone come to you without bringing this teaching, do not receive that person into your house or give a welcome.

<sup>11</sup> The one who gives such a person a welcome takes part in that person’s wicked deeds.

<sup>12</sup> I have many things to write to you, but I would prefer not to do so with paper and ink. Instead, I hope to come and speak with you face to face, so that our joy may be complete[^1-12-1].

<sup>13</sup> The children of your elect sister send you greetings.[^1-13-1]

---

[^1-3-1]: **1:3** Some manuscripts add the Lord
[^1-8-1]: **1:8** Some manuscripts read you
[^1-9-1]: **1:9** Some manuscripts read turns aside
[^1-9-2]: **1:9** Some manuscripts add of Christ
[^1-12-1]: **1:12** Some manuscripts read your
[^1-13-1]: **1:13** Some manuscripts add Amen

# Jude

## Chapter 1

<sup>1</sup> Jude,[^1-1-1] a slave[^1-1-5] of Jesus Christ and a brother of James, To those who are called, loved[^1-1-2] in[^1-1-3] God the Father and preserved for[^1-1-4] Jesus Christ:

<sup>2</sup> Mercy, peace, and love be multiplied to you.

<sup>3</sup> Beloved, while I was very eager to write to you about the salvation we share, I now find it necessary to write and urge you to contend for the faith once for all handed down to the saints.

<sup>4</sup> For certain people have slipped in among you unnoticed—people designated long ago for condemnation, the ungodly, who turn the grace of our God into a license for immorality and deny our only Master and Lord, Jesus Christ.[^1-4-1]

<sup>5</sup> I want to remind you—you have been fully informed once and for all—that Jesus delivered a people[^1-5-1] out of the land of Egypt and afterward destroyed the ones who did not believe.

<sup>6</sup> And the angels who did not stay within their own domain but abandoned their proper dwelling—these he has kept in eternal chains under darkness, bound for judgment on that great day.

<sup>7</sup> Likewise, Sodom and Gomorrah and the cities around them gave themselves up to sexual immorality and pursued unnatural desire[^1-7-1]; they are on display as an example, undergoing a punishment of eternal fire.

<sup>8</sup> Likewise these dreamers pollute the flesh, reject authority, and speak abusively of the glorious ones[^1-8-1].

<sup>9</sup> Yet the archangel Michael, in his dispute with the devil over the body of Moses, did not dare to pronounce a condemnation for slander[^1-9-1] against him; he said only, “The Lord rebuke you!”

<sup>10</sup> These people, though, speak abusively of anything beyond their understanding, and what they grasp by instinct, as irrational animals do, becomes their ruin.

<sup>11</sup> Woe to them! They travel the path of Cain, rush for profit into the error of Balaam, and perish in Korah’s rebellion.

<sup>12</sup> These people are stains[^1-12-1] on your love feasts, feasting with you without fear but shepherding only themselves[^1-12-2]. They are clouds without water, carried along by the wind; fruitless trees in autumn, twice dead after being uprooted.

<sup>13</sup> They are wild waves of the sea, foaming up their own shame; wandering stars, for whom blackest darkness has been reserved forever.

<sup>14</sup> Enoch, the seventh from Adam, also prophesied about them: “Behold, the Lord is coming[^1-14-1] with myriads of his holy ones

<sup>15</sup> to execute judgment on everyone, and to convict all the ungodly[^1-15-1] of every ungodly act of wickedness and every harsh word spoken against him by ungodly sinners.”

<sup>16</sup> These people are discontented grumblers, following their lusts[^1-16-1]; their mouths spew arrogance; they flatter others for their own advantage.

<sup>17</sup> But you, beloved, remember what was foretold by the apostles of our Lord Jesus Christ

<sup>18</sup> when they said to you, “In the last time there will be scoffers who will follow after their own ungodly desires.”

<sup>19</sup> These are the ones who cause divisions, who are worldly and devoid of the Spirit.

<sup>20</sup> But you, beloved, by building yourselves up in your most holy faith and praying in the Holy Spirit,

<sup>21</sup> keep yourselves in the love of God as you await the mercy of our Lord Jesus Christ that leads to eternal life.

<sup>22</sup> And have mercy on some who waver;

<sup>23</sup> save others by snatching them from the fire; and to still others show mercy tempered with fear, hating even the garment stained by their bodies[^1-23-1].

<sup>24</sup> Now to him who is able to keep you from stumbling and to present you unblemished in his glorious presence, with great joy—

<sup>25</sup> to the only God our Savior be glory, majesty, dominion, and authority through Jesus Christ our Lord before all time, and now, and for all eternity. Amen.

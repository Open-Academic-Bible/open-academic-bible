# 1 Peter

Chapters: [1](#chapter-1) · [2](#chapter-2) · [3](#chapter-3) · [4](#chapter-4) · [5](#chapter-5)

## Chapter 1

<sup>1</sup> Peter, apostle of Jesus Christ, To the exiles scattered throughout Pontus, Galatia, Cappadocia, Asia, and Bithynia,

<sup>2</sup> who were chosen beforehand by God the Father, sanctified by the Spirit for obedience to Jesus Christ and for sprinkling with his blood: Grace and peace be yours in abundance.

<sup>3</sup> Blessed be the God and Father of our Lord Jesus Christ! By his great mercy he has given us new birth into a living hope through the resurrection of Jesus Christ from the dead,

<sup>4</sup> and into an inheritance that is imperishable, undefiled, and unfading, reserved in heaven for you,

<sup>5</sup> who through faith are shielded by God’s power for the salvation that is ready to be revealed in the last time.

<sup>6</sup> In this you greatly rejoice[^1-6-1], though now for a little while you may have had to suffer grief in various trials

<sup>7</sup> so that the proven character of your faith—more precious than gold, which perishes even though refined by fire—may result in praise, glory, and honor at the revelation of Jesus Christ.

<sup>8</sup> Though you have not seen him[^1-8-1], you love him; and though you do not see him now, you believe in him and rejoice with an inexpressible and glorious joy,

<sup>9</sup> now that you are receiving the goal of your faith, the salvation of your souls[^1-9-1].

<sup>10</sup> Concerning this salvation, the prophets who foretold the grace to come to you searched and investigated carefully,

<sup>11</sup> trying to determine the time and setting[^1-11-1] to which the Spirit of Christ in them was pointing when he predicted the sufferings of Christ and the glories to follow.

<sup>12</sup> It was revealed to them that they were not serving themselves, but you, when they foretold the things now announced by those who preached the gospel to you by the Holy Spirit sent from heaven. Even angels long to look into these things.

<sup>13</sup> Therefore prepare your minds for action[^1-13-1]. Be sober-minded. Set your hope fully on the grace to be given you at the revelation of Jesus Christ.

<sup>14</sup> As obedient children, do not conform to the passions of your former ignorance.

<sup>15</sup> But just as he who called you is holy, so be holy in all you do,

<sup>16</sup> for it is written: “Be holy, because I am holy.”

<sup>17</sup> Since you call on a Father who judges each one’s work impartially, conduct yourselves in reverent fear during your stay as foreigners.

<sup>18</sup> For you know that it was not with perishable things such as silver or gold that you were redeemed from the empty way of life you inherited from your forebears,

<sup>19</sup> but with the precious blood of Christ, a lamb without blemish or spot.

<sup>20</sup> He was marked out before the world’s foundation, but was revealed in the last times for your sake.

<sup>21</sup> Through him you believe in God, who raised him from the dead and glorified him; and so your faith and hope are in God.

<sup>22</sup> Having purified your souls[^1-22-1] by obedience to the truth[^1-22-2], so that you have a sincere love for your brothers and sisters, love one another earnestly from the heart[^1-22-3].

<sup>23</sup> For you have been born again, not of perishable seed, but of imperishable, through the living and enduring word of God[^1-23-1].

<sup>24</sup> For, “All flesh is like grass, and all its glory like the blossom of grass; the grass withers, and the blossom falls away,

<sup>25</sup> but the word of the Lord stands forever.” And this word is the good news preached to you.

## Chapter 2

<sup>1</sup> Rid yourselves, therefore, of all malice, deceit, hypocrisy, envy, and slander.

<sup>2</sup> Like newborn babies, crave pure spiritual milk, so that through it you may grow up into salvation,

<sup>3</sup> if you have indeed tasted that the Lord is good[^2-3-1].

<sup>4</sup> As you come to him, a living stone rejected by people but chosen and precious with God,

<sup>5</sup> you too, as living stones, are to let yourselves be built up[^2-5-1] into a spiritual house for a holy priesthood, offering spiritual sacrifices that God welcomes through Jesus Christ.

<sup>6</sup> For it stands in scripture: “See, I lay in Zion a stone, a chosen and precious cornerstone; and the one who believes in him will never be put to shame.”

<sup>7</sup> So the honor belongs to you who believe, but for those who do not believe[^2-7-1], “The stone the builders rejected has become the head of the corner,”

<sup>8</sup> and, “A stone of stumbling and a rock of offense.” They stumble because they disobey the word—and to this they were appointed.

<sup>9</sup> But you are a chosen people, a royal priesthood, a holy nation, a people for God’s own possession, to proclaim the virtues of him who called you out of darkness into his marvelous light.

<sup>10</sup> Once you were not a people, but now you are the people of God; once you had not received mercy, but now you have received mercy.

<sup>11</sup> Beloved, I urge you, as foreigners and exiles, to abstain from the desires of the flesh, which war against your soul[^2-11-1].

<sup>12</sup> Keep your conduct honorable among the gentiles, so that, even if they speak against you as evildoers, they may observe your good works and glorify God on the day he comes in judgment[^2-12-1].

<sup>13</sup> Submit yourselves to every human authority[^2-13-1] for the Lord’s sake, whether to the emperor as the supreme ruler,

<sup>14</sup> or to governors as those sent by him to punish those who do wrong and to praise those who do right.

<sup>15</sup> For this is God’s will: that by doing good you put to silence the ignorance of foolish people.

<sup>16</sup> Live in freedom, but do not use your freedom as a cover-up for evil; live as slaves[^2-16-1] of God.

<sup>17</sup> Treat everyone with high regard. Love the family of believers[^2-17-1], and fear God; honor the emperor.

<sup>18</sup> Slaves, place yourselves under your masters in all respect[^2-18-1], not merely under the good and gentle ones, but also under the crooked.

<sup>19</sup> For if anyone endures the pain of unjust suffering out of consciousness of God, this is to be commended.

<sup>20</sup> How is it to your credit if you are beaten for doing wrong and you endure it? But if you suffer for doing good and you endure it, this is commendable before God.

<sup>21</sup> For to this you were called, because Christ also suffered for you, leaving you an example, that you should follow in his footsteps:

<sup>22</sup> “He committed no sin, and no deceit was found in his mouth.”

<sup>23</sup> When they heaped abuse on him, he did not retaliate; when he suffered, he made no threats, but entrusted himself to him who judges justly.

<sup>24</sup> He himself bore our sins in his own body on the cross[^2-24-1], in order that we, having died to sins, might live for righteousness. By his stripes you have been healed[^2-24-2].

<sup>25</sup> For “you were like sheep going astray,” but now you have turned back to the shepherd and overseer of your souls[^2-25-1].

## Chapter 3

<sup>1</sup> Likewise, wives, place yourselves under your own husbands, so that, even if some are disobedient to the word, they will be won over without words by the behavior of their wives

<sup>2</sup> when they see your pure and reverent demeanor.

<sup>3</sup> Your adornment should not be outward, such as braided hair or gold jewelry or fine clothes,

<sup>4</sup> but let it be the hidden person of the heart, with the imperishable beauty of a gentle and quiet spirit, which is of great worth in God’s sight.

<sup>5</sup> For this is how the holy women of the past adorned themselves. They put their hope in God and were submissive to their husbands,

<sup>6</sup> just as Sarah obeyed Abraham and called him lord. And you became her daughters, so long as you do good and refuse to give way to fear.

<sup>7</sup> Husbands, in the same way, live with your wives with consideration, giving honor to the woman as the weaker vessel[^3-7-1] and as fellow heirs of the gracious gift of life, so that your prayers will not be hindered.

<sup>8</sup> Finally, all of you, be like-minded and sympathetic, love as brothers and sisters, be tenderhearted and humble.

<sup>9</sup> Do not repay evil with evil or insult with insult, but with blessing, because to this you were called so that you may inherit a blessing.

<sup>10</sup> For, “Those who would love life and see good days must restrain their tongues from evil and keep deceitful speech from their lips.

<sup>11</sup> They must turn aside from evil and do what is good; they must seek peace and pursue it.

<sup>12</sup> For the eyes of the Lord are on the righteous, and his ears are inclined to their prayer. But the face of the Lord is against those who do evil.”

<sup>13</sup> Who can harm you if you are zealous for what is good?

<sup>14</sup> But even if you should suffer for what is right[^3-14-1], you are blessed. “Do not fear what they fear[^3-14-2]; do not be shaken.”

<sup>15</sup> But in your hearts sanctify Christ as Lord. Always be prepared to give a defense to everyone who asks you the reason for the hope that is in you. But respond with gentleness and respect,

<sup>16</sup> keeping a good conscience, so that those who abuse your good behavior in Christ may be put to shame when you are maligned[^3-16-1].

<sup>17</sup> For it is better, if it is God’s will, to suffer for doing good than for doing evil.

<sup>18</sup> Christ himself suffered for sins[^3-18-1] once and for all, the righteous on behalf of the unrighteous, so that he might lead you to God[^3-18-2]. He was put to death in the flesh, yet made alive in the spirit,

<sup>19</sup> in which he also went and proclaimed to the spirits who are in prison

<sup>20</sup> who disobeyed long ago, while God was patiently waiting in the days of Noah as the ark was being built. In the ark a few people, eight lives in all, were saved through water.

<sup>21</sup> And this water symbolizes the baptism that now saves you also—not the removal of dirt from the body, but a plea made to God, seeking a good conscience[^3-21-1]—through the resurrection of Jesus Christ,

<sup>22</sup> who has gone into heaven and is at the right hand of God, with angels, authorities, and powers having been subjected to him.

## Chapter 4

<sup>1</sup> Therefore, since Christ endured suffering in the flesh[^4-1-1], arm yourselves with the same attitude, because anyone who suffered in the flesh has ceased from sin,

<sup>2</sup> so that you no longer live out your remaining time in the flesh for human passions, but for the will of God.

<sup>3</sup> For you have spent enough time in the past carrying out the same desires as the gentiles: living in debauchery, lust, drunkenness, orgies, carousing, and unlawful idolatry.

<sup>4</sup> Because of this, they consider it strange of you not to plunge with them into the same flood of reckless indiscretion, and so they blaspheme[^4-4-1].

<sup>5</sup> But they will have to give an account to him who is ready to judge the living and the dead.

<sup>6</sup> That is why the good news was announced to dead people as well, so that, though they had been judged in the flesh as people are, they might live in the spirit as God does.

<sup>7</sup> The end of all things has come near[^4-7-1]. So be clear-minded and sober, with a view to your prayers.

<sup>8</sup> Above all, love one another deeply, because love covers over a multitude of sins.

<sup>9</sup> Show hospitality to one another without complaining.

<sup>10</sup> As good stewards of the manifold grace of God, each of you should use whatever gift you have received to serve one another.

<sup>11</sup> Anyone who speaks should speak as one conveying the words of God; anyone who serves should serve with the strength God provides, so that in all things God may be glorified through Jesus Christ, to whom be the glory and the dominion forever and ever. Amen.

<sup>12</sup> Beloved, do not be startled by the fiery trial going on among you as a test, as if something strange were happening to you.

<sup>13</sup> But rejoice to the extent that you share in the sufferings of Christ, so that you may be overjoyed at the revelation of his glory.

<sup>14</sup> If you are insulted for the name of Christ, you are blessed, since the spirit of glory[^4-14-1], even the Spirit of God, rests upon you[^4-14-2][^4-14-3].

<sup>15</sup> Indeed, none of you should suffer as a murderer or thief or wrongdoer, or even as a meddler[^4-15-1].

<sup>16</sup> But if you suffer as a Christian, do not be ashamed, but glorify God that you bear that name[^4-16-1].

<sup>17</sup> For it is time for judgment to begin with the family of God; and if it begins with us, what will the outcome be for those who disobey the gospel of God?

<sup>18</sup> And, “If it is hard for the righteous to be saved, what will become of the ungodly and the sinner?”

<sup>19</sup> So then, those who suffer according to God’s will should entrust their lives to the care of a faithful Creator and continue to do good.

## Chapter 5

<sup>1</sup> As a fellow elder, a witness of Christ’s sufferings, and a partaker of the glory to be revealed, I appeal to the elders among you:

<sup>2</sup> Be shepherds of God’s flock that is among you, watching over them[^5-2-1] not out of compulsion, but willingly, as God would have you[^5-2-2]; not out of greed, but out of eagerness;

<sup>3</sup> not lording it over those entrusted to you, but being examples to the flock.

<sup>4</sup> And at the appearing of the chief shepherd, you will receive the unfading crown of glory.

<sup>5</sup> Likewise, you younger ones, place yourselves under the elders[^5-5-1]. And all of you, put on humility toward one another, because, “God opposes the proud, but gives grace to the humble.”

<sup>6</sup> Humble yourselves, therefore, under God’s mighty hand, so that in due time he may exalt you.

<sup>7</sup> Cast all your anxiety on him, because he cares for you.

<sup>8</sup> Be sober-minded and alert. Your adversary the devil prowls around like a roaring lion, seeking someone to devour.

<sup>9</sup> Resist him, standing firm in your faith and knowing that your brothers and sisters throughout the world are going through the same sufferings.

<sup>10</sup> After you have suffered a little while, the God of all grace, who has called you to his eternal glory in Christ[^5-10-1], will himself restore you, support you, strengthen you, and ground you.

<sup>11</sup> To him be the power[^5-11-1] forever and ever. Amen.

<sup>12</sup> Through Silvanus, whom I regard as a faithful brother, I have written to you briefly, encouraging you and testifying that this is the true grace of God. Stand firm in it.

<sup>13</sup> Your sister church in Babylon[^5-13-1], chosen along with you, sends greetings, as does my son Mark.

<sup>14</sup> Greet one another with a kiss of love. Peace to all of you who are in Christ.[^5-14-1]

# 1 Timothy

Chapters: [1](#chapter-1) · [2](#chapter-2) · [3](#chapter-3) · [4](#chapter-4) · [5](#chapter-5) · [6](#chapter-6)

## Chapter 1

<sup>1</sup> Paul, an apostle of Christ Jesus by the command of God our Savior and of Christ Jesus our hope,

<sup>2</sup> To Timothy, my true child[^1-2-1] in the faith: Grace, mercy, and peace from God the Father and Christ Jesus our Lord.

<sup>3</sup> As I urged you on my departure to Macedonia, you should stay on at Ephesus to instruct certain people not to teach a different doctrine

<sup>4</sup> or devote themselves to myths and endless genealogies. These produce speculation rather than the training from God[^1-4-1] that is grounded in faith.

<sup>5</sup> The goal of this instruction is love flowing from a pure heart, from a good conscience, and from a sincere faith.

<sup>6</sup> Some have strayed from these ways and turned aside to empty talk.

<sup>7</sup> They want to be teachers of the law, but they do not understand what they are saying or that which they so confidently assert.

<sup>8</sup> Now we know that the law is good, if one uses it legitimately.

<sup>9</sup> We realize that law is not enacted for the righteous, but for the lawless and rebellious, for the ungodly and sinful, for the unholy and profane, for killers of father or mother, for murderers,

<sup>10</sup> for the sexually immoral, for men who have sex with men,[^1-10-1] for slave traders and liars and perjurers, and for whatever else is opposed to sound teaching

<sup>11</sup> that agrees with the glorious gospel of the blessed God, with which I have been entrusted.

<sup>12</sup> I thank Christ Jesus our Lord, who has strengthened me, because he considered me faithful and appointed me to service.

<sup>13</sup> I was formerly a blasphemer, a persecutor, and a violent man; yet because I had acted in ignorance and unbelief, I was shown mercy.

<sup>14</sup> And the grace of our Lord overflowed, along with[^1-14-1] the faith and love that are in Christ Jesus.

<sup>15</sup> This is a trustworthy saying, worthy of full acceptance: Christ Jesus came into the world to save sinners, of whom I am the foremost.

<sup>16</sup> But for this very reason I was shown mercy, so that Christ Jesus might display in me, as the foremost, his perfect patience, as an example for those who would later believe in him and gain eternal life.

<sup>17</sup> Now to the King of the ages, immortal, invisible[^1-17-1], the only God, be honor and glory forever and ever[^1-17-2]. Amen.

<sup>18</sup> Timothy, my child, I entrust you with this command in keeping with the previous prophecies about you, so that by them you may fight the good fight,

<sup>19</sup> holding on to faith and a good conscience. Some have rejected conscience and so have made shipwreck of their faith.

<sup>20</sup> Among them are Hymenaeus and Alexander, whom I have handed over to Satan to be taught not to blaspheme.

## Chapter 2

<sup>1</sup> First of all, then, I urge that petitions, prayers, intercessions, and thanksgiving be offered for everyone—

<sup>2</sup> for kings and all those in authority—so that we may lead tranquil and quiet lives in all godliness and dignity.

<sup>3</sup> This is good and pleasing in the sight of God our Savior,

<sup>4</sup> who wants everyone to be saved and to come to the knowledge of the truth.

<sup>5</sup> For God is one, and one also is the mediator between God and human beings, the human Christ Jesus,

<sup>6</sup> who gave himself as a ransom for all—the testimony that was given at just the right time.

<sup>7</sup> For this reason I was appointed as a herald, an apostle, and, in faith and truth, a teacher of the gentiles. I speak the truth;[^2-7-1] I am not lying.

<sup>8</sup> Therefore I want the men everywhere to pray, lifting up holy hands, without anger or dissension.

<sup>9</sup> Likewise, I want the women to adorn themselves with moderate clothing, with reverence, and with self-control, not with braided hair or gold or pearls or expensive clothes,

<sup>10</sup> but with good deeds, as befits women who profess godliness.

<sup>11</sup> A woman[^2-11-1] must learn in silence and full submission.

<sup>12</sup> I do not allow a woman[^2-12-1] to teach, nor to hold authority over a man[^2-12-2]; she must be silent.

<sup>13</sup> For Adam was formed first, and then Eve.

<sup>14</sup> And it was not Adam who was deceived, but the woman who was deceived and became a transgressor.

<sup>15</sup> She will be saved, however, through childbearing, if they remain in faith, love, and holiness, with self-control.

## Chapter 3

<sup>1</sup> This is a trustworthy saying:[^3-1-1] Whoever aspires to be an overseer desires a noble task.

<sup>2</sup> An overseer, then, must be above reproach, the husband of but one wife,[^3-2-1] temperate, self-controlled, respectable, hospitable, able to teach,

<sup>3</sup> not dependent on wine, not violent but gentle, peaceable, and free of the love of money.

<sup>4</sup> An overseer must manage his own household well and have his children respectful and submissive in all respects.

<sup>5</sup> For if someone does not know how to manage his own household, how can he care for the church of God?

<sup>6</sup> He must not be a new convert, lest he become conceited and fall into the devil’s condemnation.

<sup>7</sup> Furthermore, he must have a good reputation with outsiders, so that he will not fall into disgrace and into the snare of the devil.

<sup>8</sup> Deacons likewise must be dignified, not double-tongued or given to much wine or greedy for money.

<sup>9</sup> They must hold to the mystery of the faith with a clear conscience.

<sup>10</sup> Additionally, they must first be tested. Then, if they are above reproach, let them serve as deacons.

<sup>11</sup> In the same way, women[^3-11-1] must be dignified, not slanderers, but temperate and faithful in all things.

<sup>12</sup> A deacon must be the husband of but one wife,[^3-12-1] a good manager of his children and of his own household.

<sup>13</sup> For those who have served well as deacons acquire for themselves a high standing and great confidence in the faith that is in Christ Jesus.

<sup>14</sup> Although I hope to come to you soon, I am writing you these things

<sup>15</sup> in case I am delayed, so that you will know how people ought to conduct themselves in God’s household—the church of the living God, which is the pillar and foundation of the truth.

<sup>16</sup> Great, by common confession, is the mystery of godliness: he[^3-16-1] was revealed in flesh, was vindicated[^3-16-2] in spirit,[^3-16-3] was seen by angels, was proclaimed among the nations, was believed on in the world, was taken up in glory.

## Chapter 4

<sup>1</sup> Now the Spirit expressly states that in the latter[^4-1-1] times some will abandon[^4-1-2] the faith, giving heed to deceitful spirits and teachings of demons,

<sup>2</sup> influenced by the hypocrisy of liars, whose consciences are seared with a hot iron.

<sup>3</sup> They forbid marriage and require abstinence from certain foods that God created for believers who know the truth to receive with thanksgiving.

<sup>4</sup> For every creation of God is good, and nothing that is received with thanksgiving should be rejected,

<sup>5</sup> because it is sanctified by the word of God and prayer.

<sup>6</sup> By pointing out these things to your brothers and sisters, you will be a good servant[^4-6-1] of Christ Jesus, fed on the words of faith and the sound instruction that you have followed.

<sup>7</sup> But reject irreverent, silly myths. Instead, train yourself for godliness.

<sup>8</sup> For physical exercise is of limited value, but godliness is valuable in every way, holding promise for the present life and for the one to come.

<sup>9</sup> This is a trustworthy saying, worthy of full acceptance.

<sup>10</sup> To this end we labor and strive,[^4-10-1] because we have set our hope on the living God, who is the Savior of everyone, and especially of those who believe.

<sup>11</sup> Command and teach these things.

<sup>12</sup> Let no one despise your youth, but set an example for the believers in speech, in conduct, in love, in faith, in purity.

<sup>13</sup> Until I come, devote yourself to the public reading of scripture,[^4-13-1] to exhortation, and to teaching.

<sup>14</sup> Do not neglect the gift that is in you, which was given you through a prophetic message when the body of elders laid their hands on you.

<sup>15</sup> Be diligent in these matters and absorbed in them, so that your progress will be evident to all.

<sup>16</sup> Keep a close watch on yourself and on your teaching. Persevere in these things, for in doing so you will save yourself as well as those who hear you.

## Chapter 5

<sup>1</sup> Do not rebuke an older man[^5-1-1], but appeal to him as to a father. Treat younger men as brothers,

<sup>2</sup> older women as mothers, and younger women as sisters, with absolute purity.

<sup>3</sup> Honor the widows who are truly widows.

<sup>4</sup> But if a widow has children or grandchildren, they must first learn to show godliness to their own family and repay their parents, for this is pleasing in the sight of God.

<sup>5</sup> The widow who is truly in need and left all alone puts her hope in God and continues night and day in her petitions and prayers.

<sup>6</sup> But she who lives for pleasure is dead even while she is still alive.

<sup>7</sup> Give these instructions also, so that they will be above reproach.

<sup>8</sup> Anyone who does not provide for their own, and especially for those of their household, has disowned the faith and is worse than any unbeliever.

<sup>9</sup> A widow should be enrolled if she is at least sixty years old and has been the wife of just one husband,[^5-9-1]

<sup>10</sup> and well known for good deeds such as bringing up children, entertaining strangers, washing the feet of the saints, imparting relief to the afflicted, and devoting herself to every good work.

<sup>11</sup> But refuse to enroll younger widows. For when their passions draw them away from Christ, they will want to marry,

<sup>12</sup> and thus incur condemnation because they have broken their first pledge.

<sup>13</sup> At the same time they will also learn to be idle, going from house to house and being not only idle, but also gossips and busybodies, discussing things they should not mention.

<sup>14</sup> So I advise the younger widows to marry, have children, and manage their households, denying the adversary occasion for slander.

<sup>15</sup> For some have already turned aside to follow Satan.

<sup>16</sup> Any believing woman[^5-16-1] who has relatives that are widows must assist them and not allow the church to be burdened, so that it can help the widows who are truly in need.

<sup>17</sup> Elders who lead effectively are worthy of double honor[^5-17-1], especially those who work hard at preaching and teaching.

<sup>18</sup> For the scripture says, “Do not muzzle an ox that is treading out grain,” and, “The worker is worthy of the wages.”

<sup>19</sup> Do not entertain an accusation against an elder, except on the testimony of two or three witnesses.

<sup>20</sup> But those who persist in sin should be rebuked in front of everyone, so that the others, too, will stand in fear.

<sup>21</sup> I solemnly charge you before God and Christ Jesus and the elect angels to maintain these principles without bias, and to do nothing out of partiality.

<sup>22</sup> Do not be too quick to ordain anyone,[^5-22-1] and do not share in the sins of others. Preserve your own purity.

<sup>23</sup> Stop drinking only water and use a little wine instead, because of your stomach and your frequent ailments.

<sup>24</sup> Some people’s sins are obvious, going ahead of them to judgment, but the sins of others trail along behind them.

<sup>25</sup> In the same way, good deeds are obvious, and even the ones that are inconspicuous cannot remain hidden.

## Chapter 6

<sup>1</sup> Everyone who is a slave under the yoke should regard their masters as deserving full honor, so that God’s name and the teaching are not reviled.

<sup>2</sup> Those with believing masters should not show disrespect on account of being brothers and sisters; instead, they should serve them all the better, since those who receive their good service are beloved believers.[^6-2-1] Teach and encourage these principles.

<sup>3</sup> Whoever teaches another doctrine and disagrees with the sound words of our Lord Jesus Christ and with godly teaching,

<sup>4</sup> is conceited and understands nothing. Instead, that person has an unhealthy interest in controversies and disputes about words, out of which come envy, strife, abusive talk, evil suspicions,

<sup>5</sup> and constant friction between people of depraved mind who are devoid of the truth. They regard godliness as a means of gain.[^6-5-1]

<sup>6</sup> Of course, godliness with contentment is great gain.

<sup>7</sup> For we brought nothing into the world,[^6-7-1] so we cannot carry anything out of it.

<sup>8</sup> But if we have food and clothing, we will be content with these.

<sup>9</sup> Those who want to be rich, however, fall into temptation and become ensnared by many foolish and harmful desires that plunge them into ruin and destruction.

<sup>10</sup> For the love of money is a root of all kinds of evil. Reaching for it, some have strayed from the faith and have pierced themselves through with many sorrows.

<sup>11</sup> But you, O man of God, flee from these things and pursue righteousness, godliness, faith, love, perseverance, and gentleness.

<sup>12</sup> Fight the good fight of the faith. Take hold of the eternal life to which you were called when you made the good confession before many witnesses.

<sup>13</sup> I charge you in the presence of God, who gives life to all things, and of Christ Jesus, who made the good confession in his testimony before Pontius Pilate:

<sup>14</sup> Keep this commandment without stain or reproach until the appearance of our Lord Jesus Christ,

<sup>15</sup> which the blessed and only Sovereign—the King of kings and Lord of lords—will bring about in his own time.

<sup>16</sup> He alone has immortality and dwells in unapproachable light. No one has ever seen him, nor can anyone see him. To him be honor and eternal dominion! Amen.

<sup>17</sup> Instruct those who are rich in the present age not to be conceited and not to put their hope in the uncertainty of wealth, but in God, who richly provides all things for us to enjoy.

<sup>18</sup> Instruct them to do good, to be rich in good works, and to be generous and ready to share,

<sup>19</sup> treasuring up for themselves a firm foundation for the future, so that they may take hold of that which is truly life.

<sup>20</sup> O Timothy, guard what has been entrusted to you. Avoid irreverent, empty chatter and the opposing arguments of what is falsely called “knowledge,”

<sup>21</sup> which some have professed and thus swerved away from the faith. Grace be with you all.[^6-21-1]

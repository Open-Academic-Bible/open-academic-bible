# James

Chapters: [1](#chapter-1) · [2](#chapter-2) · [3](#chapter-3) · [4](#chapter-4) · [5](#chapter-5)

## Chapter 1

<sup>1</sup> James, a slave[^1-1-1] of God and of the Lord Jesus Christ, To the twelve tribes in the diaspora: Greetings.

<sup>2</sup> Consider it pure joy, my brothers and sisters, when you encounter trials of many kinds,

<sup>3</sup> because you know that the testing of your faith develops perseverance.

<sup>4</sup> Allow perseverance to finish its work, so that you may be complete and whole, not lacking anything.

<sup>5</sup> Now if any of you lacks wisdom, ask God, who gives generously to all and does not grudge, and it will be given to you.

<sup>6</sup> But ask in faith, with no wavering, since anyone who doubts is like a wave of the sea, blown and tossed by the wind.

<sup>7</sup> That person should not expect to receive anything from the Lord.

<sup>8</sup> Such a person is double-minded, unstable in every way.

<sup>9</sup> The brother or sister in humble circumstances should exult in their high position.

<sup>10</sup> But the one who is rich should exult in being humbled, because such a one will pass away like a flower of the field.

<sup>11</sup> For the sun rises with scorching heat and withers the plant; its flower falls and its beauty is lost. So too, the rich will fade away in the midst of a busy life.

<sup>12</sup> Blessed is anyone who endures temptation, because such a person, having stood the test, will receive the crown of life that the Lord has promised[^1-12-1] to those who love him.

<sup>13</sup> When tempted, no one should say, “I am being tempted by God.” For God cannot be tempted by evil, nor does he tempt anyone.

<sup>14</sup> But people are tempted when they are lured away and enticed by their own desire.

<sup>15</sup> Then after desire has conceived, it gives birth to sin; and sin, when it is full-grown, gives birth to death.

<sup>16</sup> Do not be deceived, my dear brothers and sisters.

<sup>17</sup> Every act of generous giving and every perfect gift is from above, coming down from the Father of lights, with whom there is no change or shifting shadow[^1-17-1].

<sup>18</sup> He chose to give us birth through the word of truth, that we would be a kind of firstfruits of his creation.

<sup>19</sup> My dear brothers and sisters, understand this: Everyone should be quick to listen, slow to speak, and slow to anger,

<sup>20</sup> for the anger of human beings does not achieve the righteousness of God[^1-20-1].

<sup>21</sup> Therefore, get rid of all moral filth and every expression of evil, and humbly accept the word planted in you, which can save your souls.

<sup>22</sup> Be doers of the word, and not hearers only. Otherwise, you are deceiving yourselves.

<sup>23</sup> For those who hear the word but do not carry it out are like people who look at their faces in a mirror[^1-23-1],

<sup>24</sup> and after observing themselves go away and immediately forget what they look like.

<sup>25</sup> But those who look intently into the perfect law of freedom, and continue to do so—not being forgetful hearers, but effective doers—such people will be blessed in what they do.

<sup>26</sup> Those who think they are religious but do not rein in their tongues are deceiving their hearts, and their religion is worthless.

<sup>27</sup> Pure and undefiled religion before our God and Father is this: to care for orphans and widows in their distress, and to keep oneself from being polluted by the world.

## Chapter 2

<sup>1</sup> Brothers and sisters, you must not mix favoritism with the faith of Jesus Christ, our Lord of glory.

<sup>2</sup> Suppose a person comes into your meeting[^2-2-1] wearing a gold ring and fine clothes, and a poor person wearing dirty clothes comes in as well.

<sup>3</sup> If you take special notice of the one in fine clothes and say, “Here is a seat of honor,” but say to the poor one, “You must stand” or “Sit under my footstool,”

<sup>4</sup> have you not discriminated among yourselves and become judges with evil thoughts?

<sup>5</sup> Listen, my dear brothers and sisters: Did God not choose the poor of this world to be rich in faith and to inherit the kingdom he promised those who love him?

<sup>6</sup> But you have dishonored the poor. Is it not the rich who oppress you and drag you into court?

<sup>7</sup> Are they not the ones who blaspheme the noble name by which you have been called?

<sup>8</sup> If you really fulfill the royal law stated in scripture, “Love your neighbor as yourself,” you are doing well.

<sup>9</sup> But if you show favoritism, you sin and are convicted by the law as transgressors.

<sup>10</sup> Whoever keeps the whole law but stumbles at just one point is answerable for all of it.

<sup>11</sup> For he who said, “Do not commit adultery,” also said, “Do not murder.” If you do not commit adultery, but do commit murder, you have become a lawbreaker[^2-11-1].

<sup>12</sup> Speak and act as those who are going to be judged by the law that gives freedom.

<sup>13</sup> For judgment without mercy will be shown to anyone who has not been merciful. Mercy triumphs over judgment.

<sup>14</sup> What good is it, my brothers and sisters, if someone claims to have faith but has no works? Can that faith save such a person?

<sup>15</sup> Suppose a brother or sister is without clothes and daily food.

<sup>16</sup> If one of you tells them, “Go in peace; stay warm and well fed,” but does not provide for their physical needs, what good is that?

<sup>17</sup> So too, faith by itself, unless it has works, is dead.

<sup>18</sup> But someone will say, “You have faith and I have works.” Show me that faith of yours without works, and I will show you my faith from my works.

<sup>19</sup> You believe that God is one. Good for you! Even the demons believe that—and shudder.

<sup>20</sup> Do you want to know, you foolish person, that faith without works is worthless[^2-20-1]?

<sup>21</sup> Was not our father Abraham[^2-21-1] justified by works when he offered up his son Isaac on the altar?

<sup>22</sup> You see that his faith was working together with his works, and his faith was completed by works.

<sup>23</sup> And the scripture was fulfilled that says, “Abraham believed God, and it was credited to him as righteousness[^2-23-1],” and he was called a friend of God.

<sup>24</sup> You can see that a person is justified by works, not by faith alone.

<sup>25</sup> In the same way, was not even Rahab the prostitute justified by her works when she received the messengers[^2-25-1] and sent them away by a different route?

<sup>26</sup> As the body without the spirit is dead, so faith without works is dead.

## Chapter 3

<sup>1</sup> Not many of you should become teachers, my brothers and sisters, because you know that we who teach will be judged more strictly.

<sup>2</sup> We all stumble in many ways. If anyone is never at fault in what is said, such a one is a mature person[^3-2-1], able to control the whole body.

<sup>3</sup> When we put bits into the mouths of horses to make them obey us, we guide their whole bodies.

<sup>4</sup> Consider ships as well. Although they are so large and are driven by strong winds, they are steered by a very small rudder wherever the pilot is inclined.

<sup>5</sup> In the same way, the tongue is a small part of the body, but it boasts of great things. Consider how small a spark sets a great forest ablaze.

<sup>6</sup> The tongue also is a fire, a world of wickedness among the parts of the body. It defiles the whole body, sets the course of life on fire, and is itself set on fire by hell[^3-6-1].

<sup>7</sup> All kinds of animals, birds, reptiles, and creatures of the sea are being tamed and have been tamed by humankind,

<sup>8</sup> but no human being can tame the tongue. It is a restless evil[^3-8-1], full of deadly poison.

<sup>9</sup> With the tongue we bless the Lord and Father[^3-9-1], and with it we curse people, who have been made in God’s likeness.

<sup>10</sup> Out of the same mouth come blessing and cursing. This should not be, my brothers and sisters!

<sup>11</sup> Can both fresh water and bitter water flow from the same spring?

<sup>12</sup> My brothers and sisters, can a fig tree grow olives, or a grapevine bear figs? Neither can a salt spring produce fresh water.

<sup>13</sup> Who is wise and understanding among you? Let that person show it by good conduct, by works done in the gentleness that comes from wisdom.

<sup>14</sup> But if you harbor bitter jealousy and selfish ambition in your hearts, do not boast, and so lie against the truth.

<sup>15</sup> Such wisdom does not come from above, but is earthly, unspiritual, demonic.

<sup>16</sup> For where jealousy and selfish ambition exist, there will be disorder and every evil practice.

<sup>17</sup> But the wisdom from above is first of all pure, then peace-loving, gentle, accommodating, full of mercy and good fruit, impartial, and sincere.

<sup>18</sup> In peace the harvest of righteousness[^3-18-1] is sown by peacemakers.

## Chapter 4

<sup>1</sup> What causes conflicts and quarrels among you? Don’t they come from the passions at war within you?

<sup>2</sup> You crave what you do not have; you kill and covet, but are unable to obtain it. You quarrel and fight. You do not have, because you do not ask.

<sup>3</sup> And when you do ask, you do not receive, because you ask with wrong motives, that you may squander it on your pleasures.

<sup>4</sup> You adulterous people![^4-4-1] Do you not know that friendship with the world is hostility toward God? Therefore, whoever chooses to be a friend of the world is made an enemy of God.

<sup>5</sup> Or do you think the scripture speaks to no purpose? Does the spirit he caused to dwell in us[^4-5-1] yearn for envy?[^4-5-2]

<sup>6</sup> But he gives us more grace. This is why it says: “God opposes the proud, but gives grace to the humble.”

<sup>7</sup> Submit yourselves, then, to God. Resist the devil, and he will flee from you.

<sup>8</sup> Draw near to God, and he will draw near to you. Cleanse your hands, you sinners, and purify your hearts, you double-minded.

<sup>9</sup> Grieve, mourn, and weep. Let your laughter be turned to mourning, and your joy to gloom.

<sup>10</sup> Humble yourselves before the Lord, and he will exalt you.

<sup>11</sup> Brothers and sisters, do not speak against one another. Anyone who speaks against a fellow believer, or judges that person, speaks against the law and judges it. And if you judge the law, you are not a doer of the law, but a judge of it.

<sup>12</sup> There is only one lawgiver and judge[^4-12-1], who is able to save and destroy. But who are you to judge your neighbor?

<sup>13</sup> Come now, you who say, “Today or tomorrow we will go to this or that city, spend a year there, carry on business, and make a profit.”

<sup>14</sup> You do not even know what will happen tomorrow! What is your life? You are a mist that appears for a little while and then vanishes.

<sup>15</sup> Instead, you ought to say, “If the Lord is willing, we will live and do this or that.”

<sup>16</sup> As it is, you boast in your proud intentions. All such boasting is evil.

<sup>17</sup> So for anyone who knows the right thing to do, yet fails to do it, it is sin.

## Chapter 5

<sup>1</sup> Come now, you who are rich, weep and wail over the misery to come upon you.

<sup>2</sup> Your riches have rotted and moths have eaten your clothes.

<sup>3</sup> Your gold and silver are corroded. Their corrosion will testify against you and consume your flesh like fire[^5-3-1]. You have hoarded treasure in the last days.

<sup>4</sup> Look, the wages you withheld by fraud from the workers who mowed your fields are crying out against you. The cries of the harvesters have reached the ears of the Lord of hosts.

<sup>5</sup> You have lived on earth in luxury and self-indulgence. You have fattened your hearts in the day of slaughter.

<sup>6</sup> You have condemned and murdered the righteous one; that one does not resist you.

<sup>7</sup> So be patient until the Lord’s coming, brothers and sisters. See how the farmer awaits the precious fruit of the soil—how patient he is over it, until the early and late rains have come.

<sup>8</sup> You, too, be patient and strengthen your hearts, because the Lord’s coming is near[^5-8-1].

<sup>9</sup> Do not complain about one another, brothers and sisters, so that you will not be judged. Look, the Judge is standing at the door!

<sup>10</sup> Brothers and sisters, take the prophets who spoke in the name of the Lord as a pattern of suffering and of patience.

<sup>11</sup> See how blessed we consider those who have persevered. You have heard of Job’s perseverance and have seen the outcome from the Lord. The Lord is full of compassion and mercy.

<sup>12</sup> Above all, do not swear, my brothers and sisters, not by heaven or earth or by any other oath. Simply let your “Yes” be yes, and your “No,” no, so that you will not fall under judgment[^5-12-1].

<sup>13</sup> Is anyone among you suffering? Let that person pray. Is anyone cheerful? Let that person sing praises.

<sup>14</sup> Is anyone among you sick? Let that person call the elders of the church, and let them pray over the sick one, anointing with oil in the name of the Lord.

<sup>15</sup> The prayer of faith, then, will save the one who is sick, and it is the Lord who will raise that person up. If that person has committed sins, forgiveness will be given.

<sup>16</sup> Therefore confess your sins to each other and pray for each other so that you may be healed. The prayer of a righteous person has great power as it works.

<sup>17</sup> Elijah was a human being just like us. He prayed earnestly that it would not rain, and it did not rain on the land for three and a half years.

<sup>18</sup> Again he prayed, and the heavens gave rain, and the earth yielded its crops.

<sup>19</sup> My brothers and sisters, if anyone among you should wander from the truth[^5-19-1] and someone should bring that person back,

<sup>20</sup> consider this: Whoever turns a sinner from the error of that way will save that person’s soul out of death and cover over a multitude of sins.
